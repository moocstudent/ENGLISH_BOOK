## Speak & Score

> 🎙️ Practise line by line in the **interactive practice** above: press **🔊 Listen** for the model, then **🎤 Speak** to read it aloud — the browser recognises what you say and scores the match, listing any missed words. Needs mic permission; use Chrome, Edge or Safari.

## Key Phrases

- **Hi, I'm Wei. It's nice to meet you.**
  *💡 Stress 'MEET'; let your voice fall at the end.*
- **I'm from Chengdu, in the south-west of China.**
  *💡 Pause slightly at the comma.*
- **I work as a software engineer at a tech company.**
  *💡 Link 'work as' → /wɜrkəz/.*
- **I've been learning English for about five years.**
  *💡 Say 'I've been' quickly and lightly.*
- **In my free time, I enjoy hiking and photography.**
  *💡 Stress the 2nd syllable: pho-TO-graphy.*
- **Could you tell me a little about yourself?**
  *💡 Let the pitch rise a little at the end.*
- **It's a pleasure to finally meet you in person.**
  *💡 'pleasure' is /ˈpleʒər/, not /pliː/.*
- **How do you two know each other?**
  *💡 Link 'know each other' smoothly.*
- **I'm really looking forward to working with you.**
  *💡 'look forward to' + -ing (working).*
- **Sorry, could you repeat your name? I didn't catch it.**
  *💡 The 't' in 'didn't' is barely pronounced.*

## Notes

- **How to practise**: press '🔊 Listen' first, then '🎤 Speak' and read the line aloud; your score is how well the recognised words match the target, and any missed words are listed.
- **Where you'll use it**: first meetings, the opening of an interview, parties, and introducing yourself in online meetings.
- **Tip**: practise the lines in a set, minding stress and end-of-sentence intonation; recognition needs mic permission — use Chrome, Edge or Safari.
