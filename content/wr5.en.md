## Task

Describe the line graph below in one paragraph, as in IELTS Writing Task 1. It shows the online sales of a coffee brand (in thousands of units) from 2018 to 2023: 2018 = 20, 2019 = 35, 2020 = 60, 2021 = 55, 2022 = 80, 2023 = 110. Begin with an overview of the main trend, then support it with specific figures, years, trend verbs and at least one comparison. Do not give reasons or opinions.

*(55–100 words)*

## Success Criteria

- Opens by summarising what the graph shows and the main trend
- Uses accurate trend verbs (rose, fell, peaked, remained stable)
- Supports the description with specific years and figures
- Includes at least one comparison (e.g. higher than, several times)

## Useful Phrases

- The line graph shows the online sales of a coffee brand between 2018 and 2023.
- Overall, sales rose significantly over the period, despite one small dip.
- In 2018, sales stood at about 20,000 units.
- They then climbed steadily, reaching a peak of 110,000 units in 2023.
- There was a slight dip in 2021, when the figure fell to 55,000.
- Sales in 2023 were more than five times higher than in 2018.

## Model Answer

> The line graph shows the online sales of a coffee brand, measured in thousands of units, between 2018 and 2023.
> 
> Overall, sales rose sharply over the period, despite one small dip. In 2018, the figure stood at just 20,000 units. It then climbed steadily to 60,000 in 2020.
> 
> In 2021, sales fell slightly to 55,000, but they soon recovered, reaching 80,000 in 2022. By 2023, they had peaked at 110,000 units, over five times the 2018 figure.

## Notes

- **Structure**: an overview first (Overall…), then the key data points, ending with the peak or low and a comparison.
- **Trend language**: verb + adverb, e.g. rose sharply, fell slightly, remained stable, reached a peak of…
