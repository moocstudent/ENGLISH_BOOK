## 原文选读 Passage

*— 改编自 Treasure Island, Robert Louis Stevenson (1883)(公版作品). Adapted from a public-domain work.*

I still remember the old seaman as clearly as if he had come only yesterday. He arrived slowly at the door of the Admiral Benbow, a large, brown man with a heavy step, his blue coat worn and stained. A long scar crossed one cheek, a dirty white line against his weathered skin, and a wooden sea-chest rolled behind him on a hand-barrow. As he came, he sang an old sailor's song in a high, cracked voice: "Yo-ho-ho, and a bottle of rum!"

He struck the door with his stick, and when my father appeared, the stranger called roughly for a glass of rum. He drank it slowly, like a man who knew his liquor well, and all the while his eyes wandered over the cliffs and up to the signboard above our door.

The old sailor seemed to like what he saw. He told my father that the quiet little bay and the comfortable inn suited him well, and, on hearing that few guests ever came, he decided at once to stay. From his pocket he counted out silver coins, enough to pay for several weeks.

But before he settled by the fire, he drew me aside. Pressing a silver fourpenny into my hand, he made me promise to watch, day and night, for a one-legged sailor, and to warn him the very moment such a man appeared. Something in his low voice made me shiver.

## 参考译文 Translation

我至今还清清楚楚地记得那个老水手,仿佛他昨天才来过一样。他慢吞吞地走到"本葆将军"客栈门前——一个高大、皮肤黝黑的男人,脚步沉重,身上那件蓝色外套又旧又脏。一道长长的刀疤横过他一边脸颊,在饱经风霜的皮肤上留下一条肮脏的白痕;一只木头水手箱由一辆手推车载着,跟在他身后。他一边走,一边用又高又哑的嗓子唱着一支古老的水手歌:"哟——嗬——嗬,再来一瓶朗姆酒!"

他用手杖敲了敲门,父亲一出来,这陌生人就粗声粗气地要了一杯朗姆酒。他慢慢地喝着,像个识货的行家,那双眼睛却始终在崖壁上游移,又抬头望向门上的招牌。

这老水手似乎挺中意眼前的一切。他对父亲说,这安静的小海湾和舒适的客栈很合他的心意;一听说这儿难得有客人来,便当即决定住下,还从口袋里数出银币,预付了好几个星期的房钱。

可是,还没在炉火旁坐定,他就把我拉到一旁,往我手里塞了一枚四便士的银币,要我答应他:日夜留神一个只有一条腿的水手,一旦那样的人出现,立刻来报信。他压低嗓门说这话时,那语气叫我不由得打了个寒战。

## 背景与理解 Notes

- **原作与作者**:《金银岛》(Treasure Island, 1883)是英国(苏格兰)作家 Robert Louis Stevenson(罗伯特·路易斯·史蒂文森)的海盗冒险名作,以少年吉姆·霍金斯(Jim Hawkins)的第一人称讲述。本段改编自小说开篇老海盗初到客栈的情节,并非原文照录。
- **主旨**:一个神秘的老海盗带着刀疤、水手箱和海盗歌住进客栈,又叮嘱少年提防"独腿水手",为全书的宝藏冒险与悬念埋下伏笔。
- **结构 / 手法**:第一人称回忆式叙述("我至今还记得");先绘外貌(刀疤、旧外套、水手箱),再写言行(唱歌、要酒、付钱、嘱托),层层铺陈悬念;结尾的"寒战"点出不祥。
- **语气**:悬疑、紧张,又带几分冒险传奇色彩;叙述冷静,却处处暗示这个陌生人藏着危险的过去。

## 阅读理解 Reading Comprehension

1. Which words best describe the feeling the old seaman brings to the inn?
- A) Cheerful and safe
- B) Mysterious and slightly threatening
- C) Sad and hopeless
- D) Funny and light-hearted

2. What did the old seaman ask the boy to do?
- A) To carry his sea-chest upstairs
- B) To bring him a map of the coast
- C) To watch for a one-legged sailor and warn him
- D) To keep his rum a secret from Father

3. What does the seaman's warning about a one-legged sailor suggest?
- A) He is looking forward to meeting an old friend.
- B) He fears someone from his past and may be in danger.
- C) He wants to hire more sailors for a voyage.
- D) He is simply making a joke with the boy.

4. In "his eyes wandered over the cliffs," the word *wandered* most nearly means
- A) closed tightly
- B) moved slowly here and there
- C) filled with tears
- D) stared angrily at one spot

5. Why does the writer end with the boy feeling a shiver at the man's low voice?
- A) To show that the room was very cold
- B) To hint that danger and secrets surround the stranger
- C) To prove that the boy was ill
- D) To explain that the man was about to sing again

**答案与解析 Answer Key**

1) **B** — 主旨·情绪题。刀疤、海盗歌、粗鲁的举止,加上提防"独腿水手"的叮嘱和结尾的寒战,共同营造出神秘而略带威胁的气氛,而非欢快(A)、绝望(C)或滑稽(D)。
2) **C** — 细节题。老海盗给"我"一枚四便士银币,要"我"留神并警告他一个"one-legged sailor"(独腿水手)的出现。
3) **B** — 推断题。他要人日夜提防一个独腿水手,说明他害怕过去认识的某个人、恐怕正身处危险之中,而非会友(A)、招募水手(C)或开玩笑(D)。
4) **B** — 词义题。"his eyes wandered over the cliffs"指目光在崖壁上缓缓游移、四处扫视,故 *wandered* 意为"慢慢地四处移动"。
5) **B** — 语气·手法题。以"我"因他压低的嗓音而打寒战收尾,暗示这个陌生人身上笼罩着危险与秘密,而非天冷(A)或"我"生病(C)。
