## 核心讲解

### 在机场

- **值机:** *I'm checking in for the 10:40 to Tokyo.*(办 10:40 飞东京的登机)/ *Just this one to check, and this as carry-on.*(这件托运,这件随身)
- **选座:** *Could I get a window seat, if there's one left?*(还有靠窗吗)
- **安检:** *Do I need to take my laptop out?* / *Shoes on or off?*
- **延误:** *Has the gate changed?*(登机口变了吗)/ *How long is the delay looking?*(大概延误多久)

### 在酒店

- **入住:** *I've got a reservation under [name].*(用……的名字订的)/ *Is early check-in possible?*(能提前入住吗)
- **请求:** *Any chance I could get a higher floor / a late check-out?*(能不能高楼层/延迟退房)
- **问题:** *The AC isn't working — could someone take a look?*(空调坏了,能来看看吗)

### 问路与出行

- **询问:** *How do I get to …?* / *Is it walkable?*(走得到吗)/ *Which line do I take?*(坐几号线)
- **听懂指路:** *take your second left*(第二个路口左转)、*it's on your right*(在你右手边)、*you can't miss it*(肯定找得到)、*ten minutes tops*(最多十分钟)
- **打车/网约车:** *Could you take me to …?* / *Do you take card?*(能刷卡吗)/ *Keep the change.*(不用找了)

### 小贴士

委婉句型让请求更自然:**Any chance I could…?**、**Would it be possible to…?**、**Could I get…?**——都比生硬的 *I want…* 更礼貌。

## 典型例句

**例 1.** 改得更礼貌: *I want a room on a higher floor.* → **Any chance I could get a room on a higher floor?**

**例 2.** *Is it ___?*(能走过去吗) → **walkable**

**例 3.** 司机: *Any bags for the ___?*(后备箱) → **trunk**(英式:*boot*)

## 练习与自测

1. 前台: *I have a reservation ___ Kim.* → **under**
2. 问要多久: *About how long ___ it take?* → **will**
3. *Forty minutes, ___ or take.* → **give**
4. 问出租车能否刷卡。 → 例: **Do you take card?**
