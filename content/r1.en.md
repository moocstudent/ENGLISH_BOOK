## Core Notes

### Purpose drives method

- **Skimming** — gist and structure (fast)
- **Scanning** — locate names, dates, numbers (fast)
- **Close reading** — language and argument (slow)

### Skimming steps

1. Title, subtitle, opening paragraph
2. First sentence of each paragraph
3. Conclusion
4. Headings and chart titles

Don't stop for every unknown word during a skim.

### Scanning

Move your eyes for **visual anchors**: capitals, digits, quotes. Read with a question in mind.

### Topic sentences

Often the **first sentence** of a paragraph states the main idea.

### Summary

Skim for structure; scan for details; read questions first in exams.

## Worked Examples

**Ex 1.** 5 minutes to see if a tech article matters → **skim** title, lead, subheads.

**Ex 2.** Find founding year and funding → **scan** for 2019, $12 million.

**Ex 3.** Main idea of a paragraph → usually the **first sentence**.

## Exercises

1. Skimming vs scanning in one sentence each.
2. Three places for main idea: **title, opening, topic sentences**.
3. Best for scanning: **B) find 1998 data**.
4. Don't look up every word while skimming because **it breaks flow and slows you down**.
