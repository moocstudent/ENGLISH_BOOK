## Transcript

**A:** Thanks for coming in today. To start, could you tell me a little about your background?

**B:** Of course. I've worked as a marketing assistant for the past three years at a small online shop.

**A:** Great. And what would you say is your biggest strength?

**B:** I'd say I'm very organised. For example, last year I ran all our email campaigns and kept every launch on schedule.

**A:** That's good to hear. Can you tell me about a time you had to deal with a difficult problem at work?

**B:** Sure. Once a product launch was nearly delayed because a supplier let us down, so I found a backup supplier in two days and we launched on time.

**A:** That's impressive. How do you usually handle tight deadlines?

**B:** I break big tasks into smaller steps and check my progress every day, so nothing is left to the last minute.

**A:** Perfect. If we offered you the role, when would you be able to start?

**B:** I'd need to give my current employer two weeks' notice, so I could start on the first of September.

**A:** That works well for us. Do you have any questions for me?

**B:** Yes — could you tell me what a typical day in this team looks like?

**A:** Absolutely. Let's go through that now, and we'll be in touch by the end of the week.

## Notes

- **Genre:** a job-interview dialogue — warm but professional in tone.
- **Key info to catch:** the years and role of experience, a strength plus an example, the behavioural question, and the start date / notice period.
- **Listening tip:** interviews often ask about your **biggest strength** and say **tell me about a time…**; watch for **give notice** and the exact date.
