## Core Notes

### Phrasal verbs

Verb + particle; meaning is often idiomatic: **look up** (search), **give up** (quit), **take off** (leave the ground).

### Transitive, intransitive, separable

- Intransitive: The plane **took off**.
- Separable: **pick up** the phone / **pick it up** (pronoun in the middle)
- Inseparable: **look after** the kids

### Collocations

Prefer **make a decision**, **take a break**, **pay attention** — learn verb+noun chunks in sentences.

### Tips

Use collocation dictionaries and corpora; don't translate word-for-word from Chinese.

## Worked Examples

**Ex 1.** look ___ the word → **up**

**Ex 2.** Fix: *do a decision* → **make a decision**

**Ex 3.** We had to ___ the meeting. → **put off**

## Exercises

1. **give up** = quit / stop trying
2. **look after** the children
3. She **gave up** smoking last year.
4. Fix: *do a choice* → **make a choice**
