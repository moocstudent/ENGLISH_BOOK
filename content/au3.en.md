Hello, this is a message for Mr. Daniel Brooks.

This is Sarah calling from the Greenfield Dental Clinic.

I'm calling about your check-up appointment, which was booked for this Thursday.

Unfortunately, the dentist is not available that day, so we need to move your appointment.

We'd like to reschedule it for Monday, the fifteenth of September, at half past two in the afternoon.

If that time doesn't suit you, please give us a call back and we'll arrange another one.

Our number is 0-1-6-3-5, 5-5-0-2-9-8.

Also, please remember to bring your insurance card with you.

And it really helps if you arrive about ten minutes early to fill in a short form.

Thank you, and we look forward to seeing you. Goodbye.

## Notes

- **Genre:** a phone voicemail (answerphone message) from a clinic — one speaker, no reply, and each fact is said only once.
- **Key info to catch:** who the message is for, why they called (a change of appointment), the NEW date + time, the callback number, and what to bring or do.
- **Listening tips:** key facts often come near the end and only once — note the number digit by digit (0-1-6-3-5…) and listen for date and time words (Monday / fifteenth / half past two).
