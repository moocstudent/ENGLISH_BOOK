## 原文选读 Passage

*— 改编自 Walden, Henry David Thoreau (1854)(公版作品). Adapted from a public-domain work.*

I went to live in the woods because I wished to live on purpose. I wanted to face only the most important facts of life, and to see whether I could learn what it had to teach. I did not want to reach the end of my days and suddenly find that I had never truly lived. Life is precious, and I did not wish to waste it on things that were not really life. I wanted to live deeply and to know life fully.

Most people, it seems to me, live in a strange hurry. They fill their days with a hundred small tasks and worries, until their lives become as rough and troubled as a stormy sea. My own advice can be said in a single word: "Simplify, simplify." Instead of a thousand small affairs, keep only two or three. When life becomes simple, the mind grows calm and clear.

We work hard to build railways, send messages, and gather the news, yet we seldom stop to ask whether these things truly matter. We have almost become servants to the very machines we made. So I built a small cabin by the pond, grew my own beans, and lived on very little. There I learned that a person needs far less than he imagines. By giving up what was unnecessary, I made room for what was real: the morning light, the quiet water, and my own honest thoughts.

## 参考译文 Translation

我到林中去生活,是因为我希望有意识地活着。我想只去面对生命中最重要的事实,看看自己能否学到它所要教给我的东西。我不愿等到生命将尽,才骤然发现自己从未真正活过。生命宝贵,我不愿把它耗费在那些并非真正生活的事情上。我要深深地活,把生命看个透彻。

在我看来,大多数人都活在一种奇怪的匆忙里。他们把日子塞满上百桩琐碎的杂务与忧虑,直到生活变得像暴风雨中的大海一样动荡不安。我自己的忠告可以用一个词说尽:"简化,再简化。"与其操办上千桩琐事,不如只留下两三件。当生活变得简单,心也就随之平静而清明。

我们辛勤地修铁路、传消息、搜集新闻,却很少停下来问一句:这些事当真重要吗?我们几乎成了自己所造机器的仆人。于是我在湖畔搭起一间小木屋,自己种豆,靠极少的东西过活。在那里我懂得:一个人所需要的,远比他所以为的要少。舍弃了多余之物,我便为真正要紧的东西腾出了空间——清晨的阳光、静谧的湖水,以及我自己诚实的思考。

## 背景与理解 Notes

- **原作与作者**:《瓦尔登湖》(Walden, 1854)是美国作家、思想家梭罗(Henry David Thoreau)的代表作,记录他在瓦尔登湖畔独居两年多、简朴生活的体验与思考(超验主义)。本段为面向学习者的改编转述,并非原文照录。
- **主旨**:入林而居,是为了"有意识地生活",直面生命最本质的事实,汲取生活的教诲,以免临终才发现自己从未真正活过——一篇关于"简朴"的宣言。
- **结构 / 手法**:第一人称自述,由"为何入林"到"简化生活"的劝告,再到简朴生活的收获;善用意象(暴风雨的海、湖畔木屋)。
- **语气**:诚恳、平和而富劝导性,对忙乱繁杂的现代生活略含批评。

## 阅读理解 Reading Comprehension

1. Why did the writer go to live in the woods?
- A) To become rich by farming
- B) To build a large new town
- C) To hide from a war
- D) To live deliberately and learn what life could teach

2. What does the writer compare the busy lives of most people to?
- A) A quiet garden
- B) A rough, stormy sea
- C) A long, easy road
- D) A warm fire

3. In the passage, "simplify" most nearly means
- A) to make something less complicated
- B) to make something more exciting
- C) to work much harder
- D) to travel far away

4. By saying "a person needs far less than he imagines," the writer suggests that
- A) people should buy more things
- B) farming is the only good life
- C) much of what people chase is unnecessary
- D) money brings happiness

5. What is the writer's attitude toward the hurried, complicated life of most people?
- A) He admires it and wants more of it
- B) He is confused by it
- C) He does not care about it
- D) He is critical of it and prefers simple living

**答案与解析 Answer Key**

1) **D** — 主旨题。首段说他入林是为"live on purpose"(有意识地生活),想"face only the most important facts of life"并"learn what it had to teach",故选 B。
2) **B** — 细节题。原文把多数人忙乱的生活比作"as rough and troubled as a stormy sea"(如暴风雨中的海一般动荡),故选 C。
3) **A** — 词义题。下文紧接"keep only two or three""the mind grows calm and clear",可见 simplify 意为"化繁为简",故选 B。
4) **C** — 推断题。他说"a person needs far less than he imagines",并靠极少之物生活,暗示人们所追逐的多属多余,故选 D。
5) **D** — 语气·手法题。他批评现代人"live in a strange hurry",劝人"Simplify, simplify",态度是批判并倡导简朴,故选 C。
