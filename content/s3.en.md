## Core Notes

### Basic passive: be + past participle

The **object** of an active sentence becomes the **subject**; the doer follows **by** and is often dropped.

- *Someone stole my bike.* → *My bike **was stolen** (by someone).*

Drop the *by*-phrase when the doer is unknown, obvious, or unimportant: *English **is spoken** here.*

### Passive in every tense

| Tense | Active | Passive |
|---|---|---|
| present simple | makes | is / are made |
| present continuous | is making | is / are being made |
| past simple | made | was / were made |
| present perfect | has made | has / have been made |
| past perfect | had made | had been made |
| future | will make | will be made |
| modal | must make | must be made |

The **past participle never changes** — only *be* and the auxiliaries do the work.

### Verbs with no passive

Intransitive verbs (happen, arrive, occur, exist, rise, die) have **no object → no passive**.

- ✗ An accident was happened. → ✓ An accident **happened**.

Some state verbs (have, fit, suit, cost, lack, resemble) also avoid the passive. Double-object verbs allow two passives: *I **was given** a book* / *A book **was given** to me*.

### get-passive & causative

- **get-passive** (informal, often bad luck): *He **got fired**. / My phone **got stolen**.*
- **have / get sth done** (someone does it for you): *I **had my hair cut**.*
- **Perception / report passive:** bare infinitive → *to*: *We saw him leave* → *He **was seen to leave***; *It **is said that**… / He **is said to be**…*

### Active vs passive — choosing

| Context | Prefer |
|---|---|
| doer unknown or unimportant | passive |
| emphasis on the result | passive |
| science / formal / news | passive |
| everyday, direct, concise | active |

English writing generally prefers the **active**; use the passive only for the cases above.

### Pitfalls

- ✗ was happened → **happened** (intransitive).
- ✗ was build → was **built** (participle, not base form).
- ✗ I am born in 1998 → I **was born**.
- Drop a vague *by someone*: *My wallet was stolen.*

### Summary

be (in the right tense) + past participle; participle fixed; no passive for intransitives; passive for unknown doer / formal / scientific writing; get-passive is informal; *have sth done* = causative.

## Worked Examples

**Example 1.** Make passive: *They are building a new bridge.* → **A new bridge is being built.**

**Example 2.** The results ___ (announce) tomorrow. → **will be announced**

**Example 3.** Fix: *An accident was happened.* → **An accident happened.**

**Example 4.** Translate: 这座寺庙建于三百多年前。 → **This temple was built more than 300 years ago.**

**Example 5.** Causative: "I had someone repair my computer." → **I had my computer repaired.**

## Exercises

1. Make passive: *Someone has cleaned the room.* → **The room has been cleaned.**
2. Rice ___ (grow) in many Asian countries. → **is grown**
3. Fix: *The window was break by the storm.* → **was broken**
4. This form must ___ (complete) in ink. → **be completed**
5. Translate: 会议被推迟到下周了。 → **The meeting was postponed until next week.**
6. Right or wrong: *The bus was arrived late.* → **Wrong; arrive is intransitive → The bus arrived late.**

**Answer key.** 1 has been cleaned · 2 is grown · 3 was broken · 4 be completed · 5 was postponed · 6 wrong (arrived).
