## 原文选读 Passage

*— Aesop's Fables, tr. George Fyler Townsend (1887). Public domain.*

**The Hare and the Tortoise**

A Hare one day ridiculed the short feet and slow pace of the Tortoise, who replied, laughing: "Though you be swift as the wind, I will beat you in a race." The Hare, believing her assertion to be simply impossible, assented to the proposal; and they agreed that the Fox should choose the course and fix the goal. On the day appointed for the race the two started together. The Tortoise never for a moment stopped, but went on with a slow but steady pace straight to the end of the course. The Hare, lying down by the wayside, fell fast asleep. At last waking up, and moving as fast as he could, he saw the Tortoise had reached the goal, and was comfortably dozing after her fatigue.

*Slow but steady wins the race.*

**The Shepherd's Boy and the Wolf**

A Shepherd's Boy, who watched a flock of sheep near a village, brought out the villagers three or four times by crying out, "Wolf! Wolf!" and when his neighbours came to help him, laughed at them for their pains. The Wolf, however, did truly come at last. The Shepherd's boy, now really alarmed, shouted in an agony of terror: "Pray, do come and help me; the Wolf is killing the sheep;" but no one paid any heed to his cries, nor rendered any assistance. The Wolf, having no cause of fear, at his leisure lacerated or destroyed the whole flock.

*There is no believing a liar, even when he speaks the truth.*

## 参考译文 Translation

**龟兔赛跑**

一天,兔子嘲笑乌龟腿短、走得慢。乌龟笑着回答:"就算你快得像风,我也能在比赛中赢过你。"兔子认为这种说法根本不可能,便答应了这个提议;他们约定由狐狸来选定路线、确定终点。到了约定比赛的那天,两个一起出发。乌龟一刻也不停歇,以缓慢却稳定的步伐一直走到路线的尽头。兔子却躺在路边,酣然睡去。等它终于醒来、拼命往前跑时,却看见乌龟早已到达终点,正舒舒服服地打着盹,休息它的疲劳呢。

*缓慢却稳定的一方赢得比赛。*

**牧童与狼**

一个牧童在村子附近看守一群羊,他三番四次高喊"狼来了!狼来了!"把村民都引了出来;等邻居们赶来帮忙,他却嘲笑他们白跑一趟。然而,狼最终真的来了。牧童这下真的慌了,惊恐万分地大喊:"求求你们,快来帮帮我;狼正在咬死羊呢!"可是没有一个人理会他的呼喊,也没有人来帮忙。狼没有什么可害怕的,便从容不迫地咬伤、咬死了整群羊。

*说谎的人,即使说了真话,也没有人相信。*

## 背景与理解 Notes

- **体裁 / 出处**:伊索寓言源自古希腊口头传统,后世结集成书;此处采用 George Fyler Townsend 1887 年经典英译本。每则寓言篇幅极短,以动物拟人,结尾用一句斜体格言点明寓意(moral)。
- **主旨**:《龟兔赛跑》说明持之以恒、稳扎稳打胜过恃才自负的懈怠;《牧童与狼》说明惯于说谎的人会失去信用,到了真需要帮助时反而无人相信。
- **结构 / 手法**:两则都采用"极简叙事 + 末句点题"的固定结构;手法上依赖拟人和对比(快与慢、谎言与真话)。
- **语气**:叙述客观、克制,近乎训诫;结尾格言语气庄重,带有明确的劝诫意味。

## 阅读理解 Reading Comprehension

1. What is the main lesson of "The Hare and the Tortoise"?
- A) Fast runners always win races.
- B) Steady, continuous effort beats careless overconfidence.
- C) It is unwise to accept a challenge from a weaker rival.
- D) Animals should not compete against one another.

2. In the first fable, who was chosen to set the course and fix the goal?
- A) The Fox
- B) The Tortoise
- C) The Hare
- D) The villagers

3. Why did no one come to help when the boy finally shouted that the Wolf was really there?
- A) The villagers were too far away to hear him.
- B) They had grown tired and gone home for the night.
- C) His earlier false alarms had destroyed their trust in him.
- D) They were afraid of being attacked by the Wolf.

4. In the sentence "the Hare... assented to the proposal," the word *assented* most nearly means
- A) agreed
- B) objected
- C) listened
- D) hurried

5. What is the purpose of the italic sentence at the end of each fable?
- A) To describe what happens next in the story.
- B) To introduce the animals who appear in it.
- C) To question whether the events could really happen.
- D) To state, in plain words, the moral the story teaches.

**答案与解析 Answer Key**

1) **B** — 主旨题。乌龟"never for a moment stopped... slow but steady pace"最终获胜,而兔子因自信"fell fast asleep",结尾格言"Slow but steady wins the race"直接点明主旨。其余选项均与情节或寓意不符。
2) **A** — 细节题。原文明确:"they agreed that the Fox should choose the course and fix the goal.",故为狐狸。
3) **C** — 推断题。牧童此前多次谎报"Wolf! Wolf!"并"laughed at them",村民因而不再信他;故真狼来时"no one paid any heed"。结尾格言也印证:说谎者说真话也无人信。
4) **A** — 词义题。兔子认为乌龟的说法不可能,于是"assented to the proposal"即"答应/同意了提议";下一句"the two started together"也说明比赛达成一致。故 assented ≈ agreed。
5) **D** — 语气·手法题。每则末尾的斜体句(如"Slow but steady wins the race.")是寓言的 moral,用直白的话点明教训,是伊索寓言的固定手法,而非叙事或提问。
