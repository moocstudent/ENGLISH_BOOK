## Core Notes

### Present Perfect

Links a past action to **now** — the result or state matters, not exactly when it happened.

**Uses:** life experience (*I have visited* Japan twice); present result (*She has lost* her keys); a state up to now (*We have lived* here for ten years); recent action (*I have just finished*).

**Form:** **have / has + past participle**. Signals: ever, never, already, yet, just, so far, recently.

### for / since

| Word | Use | Example |
|---|---|---|
| for | a length of time | for five years |
| since | a starting point | since 2019 |

**already** (positive: *I have already eaten*); **yet** (negative/question: *Have you finished yet?*); **just** (*The train has just left*).

### Past Perfect

The "past before the past": an action earlier than another past action. **Form: had + past participle.**

*When I arrived, the train had already left.* (the train left first → had left; I arrived later → past simple). Common with after, before, by the time, when.

### Present perfect vs past simple

| Situation | Tense | Example |
|---|---|---|
| definite past time | past simple | I **saw** him yesterday. |
| no time, experience/result | present perfect | I **have seen** that film. |

With yesterday, last week, in 2020, ago → past simple, not present perfect. ✗ I have seen him yesterday. → ✓ I **saw** him yesterday.

### British vs American

British keeps present perfect with just/already/yet (*I have just eaten*); American often uses past simple (*I just ate*). For exams, follow the British pattern.

### Summary

Present perfect = past action + present link. for = period, since = starting point. Past perfect = the past before the past. A clear past-time word forces past simple.

## Worked Examples

**Example 1.** I ___ (never/eat) sushi before I ___ (go) to Tokyo last year.

> **Answer.** had never eaten / went

**Example 2.** Fix: *She has bought a car last week.*

> **Answer.** She **bought** a car last week.

**Example 3.** Translate: 我认识她十年了。

> **Answer.** I **have known** her **for** ten years.

**Example 4.** By the time we arrived, the film ___ . (started / had started) → **had started**

## Exercises

1. They ___ (live) in London since 2015. → **have lived**
2. ___ you ever ___ (be) to Canada? → **Have … been**
3. Fix: *I have seen this movie two days ago.* → **I saw**
4. for or since: We've been friends ___ high school. → **since**
5. Translate: 火车刚刚离开。 → **The train has just left.**
