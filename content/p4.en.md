## Core Notes

### Opening hook

The first 30 seconds decide attention. **Hook first, then give the map:**

- **Hooks:** a question (*Have you ever…?*), a surprising fact, a short story, a quote.
- **Greet + topic:** *Good morning. Today I'd like to talk about…*
- **Roadmap:** *I've divided this into three parts: first…, then…, finally…*
- **Questions:** *Feel free to interrupt, or I'll take questions at the end.*

### Signposting the body

The audience can't see your outline — guide them with **signposts**:

| Function | Signpost |
|---|---|
| Start | *Let's start with… / First of all…* |
| Transition | *Moving on to… / This brings me to…* |
| Emphasis | *The key point here is…* |
| Refer back | *As I mentioned earlier…* |

One point at a time; don't read the slides — talk to the key words.

### Describing charts

Say the **trend + the degree**:

| Trend | Verbs | Nouns |
|---|---|---|
| Up | rise, increase, climb | a rise, an increase |
| Down | fall, drop, decline, plummet | a fall, a drop |
| Flat | level off, stay flat | a plateau |

- **Adverbs:** sharply, gradually, slightly, steadily.
- **Introduce:** *As you can see… / The graph shows… / There was a sharp rise in sales in Q3.*
- Two patterns: *Sales rose sharply* or *There was a sharp rise in sales.*

### Conclusion & call to action

- **Signal the end:** *To sum up… / In conclusion…*
- **Recap** 2–3 key messages — no new content.
- **Call to action:** *So my ask is simple: let's… / The next step is…*
- **Q&A:** stall with *That's a great question…*; check with *If I understand you correctly…*; if you don't know, *I don't have that to hand, but I'll follow up.*

### Rehearsal checklist

| Check | Point |
|---|---|
| Rehearse aloud | full run 2–3 times |
| Time it | leave room for Q&A |
| No script | key-word notes only |
| Pace & pauses | slow down, pause on key points |
| Eye contact | look at people, not the screen |

When nervous, slow down — speed wrecks stress and chunking.

### Summary

Hook then map; signpost each section; describe charts with trend + degree; recap and give a call to action; rehearse aloud with eye contact and a steady pace.

## Worked Examples

**Example 1.** Opening: *Have you ever worked in your pyjamas? Today I'll cover the benefits, the risks, and how to do it well.*

**Example 2.** Transition: *OK, next slide* → *That covers the costs — this brings me to the benefits.*

**Example 3.** Chart: Q3 up 20% → *As you can see, there was a sharp rise in sales in Q3 — up twenty percent.*

**Example 4.** Unknown Q&A: *I don't have that to hand, but I'll follow up after the talk.*

**Example 5.** Close + CTA: *To sum up: cheaper, faster, greener. So my ask is simple — let's run a three-month pilot. Thank you — questions?*

## Exercises

1. Write one question hook and one fact hook.
2. Link two sections: finish costs, move to benefits.
3. "Profits fell gradually" — write a verb version and a noun version.
4. Reply well to a question you can't answer.
5. Write a closing line with a call to action.
6. List three items from the rehearsal checklist.

**Answer key.** 1: *Have you ever…?* / *One in three people…*. 2: *That's it for costs — let's turn to the benefits.* 3: *Profits fell gradually.* / *There was a gradual fall in profits.* 4: *Great question — I'll check and get back to you.* 5: *So let's start next Monday.* 6: rehearse aloud / time it / no script / eye contact / slow pace (any three).
