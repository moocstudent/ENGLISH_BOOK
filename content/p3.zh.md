## 核心讲解

### 观点表达句型

表达看法时,先用**观点信号词**开头,让人知道这是你的意见而非事实。语气可强可弱:

| 语气 | 句型 |
|---|---|
| 中性 | *In my opinion… / I think… / I'd say…* |
| 委婉 | *It seems to me that… / As far as I'm concerned… / I tend to think…* |
| 强烈 | *I'm convinced that… / There's no doubt that… / I firmly believe…* |

- **区分事实与观点:** *The report says sales fell*(事实)对 *I think sales will keep falling*(观点)。
- **紧跟理由:** 观点后接 *because / since / the reason is…*,别只抛结论。

### 同意与部分同意

- **完全同意:** *Exactly. / That's a good point. / I couldn't agree more. / You're absolutely right.*
- **部分同意(先肯定再保留):** *I see your point, but… / That's true to some extent… / I agree up to a point, however… / Fair enough, though…*
- **技巧:** 部分同意既保持礼貌,又为表达不同看法留出空间,是讨论里最好用的过渡。

### 反驳与让步

- **礼貌不同意:** *I'm not sure I agree. / I see it differently. / With respect, I don't think that's the whole picture.*(避免生硬的 *You're wrong.*)
- **让步 + 反驳(concession):** 先承认对方有理,再转折——
  - *That may be true, but…*
  - *Even so, …*
  - *Yes, but on the other hand…*
- **用例子支撑:** *For instance… / Take … as an example / Look at what happened when…* 观点有例子才有说服力。

### 小组讨论角色

讨论是合作,不是独白。常见职责与话轮控制:

| 角色 | 任务 | 常用语 |
|---|---|---|
| 主持(chair) | 推进、点名 | *Let's get started. / What do you think, Mei?* |
| 发言者 | 贡献观点 | *Could I add something? / Building on that…* |
| 计时/总结 | 控场、收束 | *We're short on time. / So, to sum up…* |

- **邀请他人:** *What's your take on this? / Would you like to add anything?*
- **争取发言:** *Could I just come in here? / Can I finish my point?*
- **复述再回应:** *So what you're saying is… / If I understand you correctly…* 先总结对方,再表明立场,显得你在听。
- **别垄断:** 说完主动把话轮交出去,给安静的人机会。

### 辩论赛基本结构

正式辩论围绕一个**辩题(motion)**,正方(proposition)支持、反方(opposition)反对:

| 阶段 | 内容 |
|---|---|
| 开场 | 定义辩题、亮出立场 |
| 立论 | 论点 + 理由 + 例子(point–reason–example) |
| 反驳 | 针对对方论点逐条回应 |
| 总结 | 重申立场、收束 |

- **信号词:** *My first argument is… / Furthermore… / To sum up, our position is…*
- **一分钟立论模板:** 观点句 → *because* 理由 → *for example* 例子 → *therefore* 重申。

### 小结

- 先用观点信号词表态,再给理由,别把观点当事实。
- 部分同意(*I see your point, but…*)最礼貌又灵活。
- 反驳先让步、后转折,并用例子支撑。
- 小组讨论要邀请、复述、控话轮,不垄断。
- 辩论用「论点—理由—例子」立论,信号词分段。

## 典型例句

**例 1.** 句型套用(委婉表达观点):

> **题目.** 把 *Remote work is better.* 改成有理由、语气委婉的观点句。
>
> **参考.** *It seems to me that remote work is better, mainly because it saves commuting time.*

**例 2.** 流利表达改写(生硬反驳 → 礼貌):

> **原句.** *You're wrong. Cars are bad.*
>
> **解.** *I see your point, but I'm not sure cars are the whole problem — public transport matters too.*

**例 3.** 让步 + 反驳:

> **任务.** 用 concession 回应 *Money brings happiness.*
>
> **参考.** *That may be true up to a point, but beyond a certain income it seems to make little difference.*

**例 4.** 迷你对话(小组话轮):

> **任务.** 邀请安静的组员发言,并接话。
>
> **参考.** A: *We've heard from Sam — Lena, what's your take?* B: *Could I just build on Sam's point?*

**例 5.** 汇报/立论片段(point–reason–example):

> **任务.** 就「学校应否禁手机」立一个论点。
>
> **参考.** *My first argument is that phones distract students. The reason is simple: notifications break focus. For example, a class that banned phones saw better attention.*

## 练习与自测

1. 写出两个「委婉」和一个「强烈」的观点句型。
2. 把 *No, that's stupid.* 改成礼貌的不同意表达。
3. 用 *That may be true, but…* 回应 *English is the only language worth learning.*
4. 写一句邀请他人发言、一句争取自己发言的话。
5. 用 *So what you're saying is…* 复述并回应一个观点。
6. 就任一辩题写「论点 + 理由 + 例子」三句。

**参考答案.**

1) 例:*It seems to me… / I tend to think…*(委婉);*I'm convinced that…*(强烈)。  2) 例:*I see it differently — I'm not sure that works.*  3) 例:*That may be true, but knowing a local language often matters more day to day.*  4) 例:*What's your take, Yu?* / *Could I just come in here?*  5) 略,须先复述后回应。  6) 略,自查三段结构是否齐全。
