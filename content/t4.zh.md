## 核心讲解

### 将来时的三种常见表达

英语没有单一的「将来时」,主要靠三种方式:

| 形式 | 主要含义 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| will + 原形 | 当下决定、预测、承诺 | I *will help* you. / It *will rain* tomorrow. |
| be going to + 原形 | 计划、意图、有迹象的预测 | We *are going to* buy a house. |
| 现在进行 | 已定好的安排(带时间) | I *am meeting* Sam at six. |

**对比要点:**

- **will**:说话瞬间才决定 — The phone's ringing. I *'ll* get it.
- **be going to**:事先已有打算 — I *'m going to* start a course next month.
- **有当前证据的预测**用 be going to:Those clouds are dark — it *'s going to* pour.
- **时刻表或日程**用一般现在:The film *starts* at 7.

### 情态动词:能力、许可、义务、推测

| 功能 | 情态动词 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| 能力 | can / could | She *can* swim. / I *could* read at four. |
| 许可 | can / may / could | *May* I come in? |
| 义务或必要 | must / have to | You *must* wear a helmet. |
| 建议 | should / ought to | You *should* rest. |
| 推测(可能) | may / might / could | He *might* be at home. |
| 推测(肯定) | must | She *must* be tired. |
| 推测(否定) | can't | That *can't* be true. |

**形式要点:** 情态动词后接**动词原形**,不加 -s、不加 to(have to / ought to 除外);否定直接加 not(can't, mustn't, shouldn't)。

### 半情态 have to / need to

- **have to** = 外部要求的义务(客观):I *have to* wear a uniform at work.
- **must** = 说话人主观强调:You *must* see this film!(强烈推荐)
- **否定差异是关键:**

| 表达 | 含义 |
|---|---|
| mustn't | 禁止(绝不可以) |
| don't have to | 不必(可做可不做) |
| don't need to / needn't | 不需要 |

**例:** You *mustn't* park here.(禁止) 不等于 You *don't have to* park here.(不必,但可以)

### 委婉建议与拒绝

**提建议(由弱到强):**

- You *could* try restarting it.(最委婉)
- Maybe you *should* talk to her.
- You *ought to* see a doctor.
- Why don't you …? / How about …?

**礼貌请求:**

- *Could* you help me? / *Would* you mind opening the window?

**委婉拒绝:**

- I'm afraid I *can't* make it.
- I'd love to, but I *have to* work.

### 写作与口语中的语气选择

| 语境 | 倾向 |
|---|---|
| 正式(邮件、报告) | would, could, may;完整拼写 |
| 非正式(朋友、口语) | 'll, can;口语才用 gonna |
| 请求帮助 | Could/Would you …? 比 Can you …? 更礼貌 |
| 表达把握程度 | must(很确定) 强于 may/might/could(不确定) |

**建议:** 正式写作**不用缩写**(用 will not 而非 won't),不用 gonna/wanna;口语可放松。

### 常见中式错误

1. ✗ I will can help you. → ✓ I *will be able to* help you.(两个情态不连用)
2. ✗ He must to go now. → ✓ He *must go* now.(must 后不加 to)
3. ✗ You don't must smoke here. → ✓ You *mustn't* smoke here.
4. ✗ Maybe it will rain, look at the clouds. → ✓ Look at the clouds — it's *going to* rain.(有迹象用 be going to)

### 小结

- 将来:will(临时决定/预测)、be going to(计划/有迹象)、现在进行(定好的安排)。
- 情态动词后接原形;表能力、许可、义务、建议、推测。
- must 与 have to 是主观对客观;mustn't(禁止)不等于 don't have to(不必)。
- 语气随语境:正式用 would/could/may 且不缩写。

## 典型例句

**例 1.** 选择 will 或 be going to:

> **题目.** A: The bags look heavy. B: I ___ help you. — We ___ visit Rome next summer; we've already booked.
>
> **解.** 'll help(当场决定,用 will) / are going to visit(已计划,用 be going to)。

**例 2.** 改错:

> **原句.** You must to finish the report by Friday.
>
> **解.** You *must finish* the report by Friday.
> must 后直接跟动词原形,不加 to。

**例 3.** 翻译:

> **中文.** 你不必来得那么早,但绝不能迟到。
>
> **解.** You *don't have to* come so early, but you *mustn't* be late.

**例 4.** 选择:

> **题目.** The lights are off. They ___ be at home.
> A) can't  B) mustn't  C) don't have to
>
> **解.** A) can't — 否定推测「不可能」用 can't。

**例 5.** 写作:

> **任务.** 用两种将来表达和一个情态动词,写 3 句你的周末计划与一条建议。
>
> **参考.** I am meeting my friends on Saturday. We are going to watch a film. If you're free, you should join us.

## 练习与自测

1. 填空: Look at those clouds! It ___ (rain).
2. 填空(已预约): I ___ (see) the dentist at 3 p.m. tomorrow.
3. 改错: I think I will can come to the party.
4. 选择: You ___ touch that wire — it's dangerous. (mustn't / don't have to)
5. 用 could 和 should 给「睡不好」的朋友提两条建议。
6. 翻译: 她一定很累,工作了一整天。

**参考答案.**

1) 's going to rain  2) am seeing  3) I think I *will be able to* come.  4) mustn't  5) 例:You could try going to bed earlier. / You should avoid coffee at night.  6) She *must be* tired — she *has worked* / *has been working* all day.
