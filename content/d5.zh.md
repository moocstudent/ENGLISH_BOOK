## 原文选读 Passage

*— 改编自 Around the World in Seventy-Two Days, Nellie Bly (1890)(公版作品). Adapted from a public-domain work.*

The idea came to me one restless evening: why not try to travel around the whole world faster than the hero of Jules Verne's famous novel? In the story, Phileas Fogg needed eighty days. I felt sure that a real person, in a real world of fast ships and trains, could do it in less.

The next morning I carried my plan to my editor. He listened, then shook his head. It was a clever idea, he agreed, but only a man could carry it out. A man could look after himself, travel light, and leave at a moment's notice. A woman, he said, would need a dozen trunks and someone to protect her, and would surely be too slow.

I refused to accept this. "Start the man," I told him, "and I will beat him." He studied my face for a long moment, and at last he smiled. The assignment was mine.

There was little time to prepare. I ordered one strong, plain dress that could be worn for months, and I packed a single small bag with my own hands. Into it went only what I truly needed — a few clothes, some money, and my courage. No trunks, no maid, no companion.

On a grey November morning I stood on the deck of the ship as it pulled slowly away from New York. Ahead of me lay wide oceans, strange cities, and thousands of miles. Alone, with my one small bag, I set out to race around the world.

## 参考译文 Translation

一个心神不宁的夜晚,这个念头闯进我脑海:何不试着比儒勒·凡尔纳那部名著里的主人公更快地环游整个世界呢?书里,菲利亚斯·福格用了八十天。我确信,在这个有快船和火车的真实世界里,一个真实的人能用更短的时间做到。

第二天早上,我把计划带去见我的主编。他听完,摇了摇头。他承认这主意很聪明,但只有男人才能完成。他说,男人能照顾自己、轻装上路、说走就走;而一个女人则需要十来只大行李箱和一个保护她的人,肯定会太慢。

我不肯接受这种说法。"让那个男人出发,"我对他说,"我会赢过他。"他久久地端详着我的脸,最后笑了。这项任务归我了。

准备的时间不多。我定制了一条结实、朴素、能连穿好几个月的裙子,又亲手收拾了一只小小的旅行包。包里只装真正需要的东西——几件衣服、一些钱,还有我的勇气。没有大行李箱,没有女仆,没有同伴。

在一个灰蒙蒙的十一月清晨,我站在轮船甲板上,看着它缓缓驶离纽约。前方是辽阔的海洋、陌生的城市和数千英里的路程。我孤身一人,带着那只小小的旅行包,出发去与整个世界赛跑。

## 背景与理解 Notes

- **原作与作者**:《环球七十二天》(1890)是美国先驱女记者 Nellie Bly(内莉·布莱,真名 Elizabeth Cochran)的旅行纪实报道。她受凡尔纳小说《八十天环游地球》启发,真的独自环球旅行并打破了"八十天"的纪录。本段为面向学习者的改写。
- **主旨**:一位年轻女记者不顾"女人做不到"的偏见,只带一只小包,独自出发环游世界,以行动证明自己。
- **结构 / 手法**:第一人称叙述,按"萌生念头—主编质疑—据理力争—轻装准备—启程"推进;以主编的怀疑反衬"我"的果决,是真实新闻叙事(narrative journalism)的范例。
- **语气**:自信、果敢,略带挑战世俗成见的锋芒。

## 阅读理解 Reading Comprehension

1. What is the main idea of the passage?
- A) A novel by Jules Verne is better than real travel.
- B) A young woman reporter sets out alone to race around the world.
- C) Travelling by ship from New York is very dangerous.
- D) Editors rarely listen to any new ideas.

2. How much luggage did the narrator finally take with her?
- A) A dozen trunks
- B) One small bag
- C) Nothing at all
- D) A large suitcase and a maid

3. Why did the editor doubt the plan at first?
- A) He thought the world was simply too large to cross.
- B) He believed a woman travelling alone would be too slow and need too much.
- C) He did not trust Jules Verne's novel.
- D) He wanted to make the journey himself.

4. In "a man could ... travel light," the phrase *travel light* most nearly means
- A) to travel only in daylight
- B) to travel with very little luggage
- C) to travel very cheaply
- D) to travel extremely quickly

5. What does the narrator's reply, "Start the man, and I will beat him," mainly show about her?
- A) Her fear of failing
- B) Her confidence and determination
- C) Her wish to travel with a companion
- D) Her admiration for Phileas Fogg

**答案与解析 Answer Key**

1) **B** — 主旨题。全段讲"我"这位女记者不顾质疑,独自轻装出发去比小说主人公更快地环游世界。A、C、D 均非主线。
2) **B** — 细节题。原文说"I packed a single small bag",且强调"No trunks, no maid, no companion";大行李箱是主编的设想(A),并非事实。
3) **B** — 推断题。主编认为只有男人能完成,女人"would need a dozen trunks and someone to protect her, and would surely be too slow",即怕她太慢、累赘太多。
4) **B** — 词义题。与"a dozen trunks"(一堆大箱子)相对,travel light 指"轻装、少带行李"。
5) **B** — 语气·手法题。面对质疑,"我"回敬"让那个男人出发,我会赢过他",尽显自信与决心;其后主编微笑并把任务交给她,也印证了这一点。
