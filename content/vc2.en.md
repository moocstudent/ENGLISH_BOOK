## Word List

| Word / phrase | POS | IPA | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| analyse | v | /ˈænəlaɪz/ | examine something in detail | We need to analyse the data before writing the report. |
| significant | adj | /sɪɡˈnɪfɪkənt/ | important or large enough to notice | There was a significant increase in sales this year. |
| approach | n | /əˈprəʊtʃ/ | a way of dealing with something | The teacher tried a new approach to explain the topic. |
| factor | n | /ˈfæktə/ | one thing that influences a result | Cost is an important factor when choosing a phone. |
| evidence | n | /ˈevɪdəns/ | facts that support a claim | The scientists found strong evidence to support the theory. |
| consequence | n | /ˈkɒnsɪkwəns/ | a result or effect of an action | One consequence of the rain was the flooded road. |
| establish | v | /ɪˈstæblɪʃ/ | set up or firmly prove | The company was established more than fifty years ago. |
| demonstrate | v | /ˈdemənstreɪt/ | show clearly | The results demonstrate that the method really works. |
| sufficient | adj | /səˈfɪʃənt/ | enough for a purpose | We did not have sufficient time to finish the test. |
| however | adv | /haʊˈevə/ | but; nevertheless | The plan was risky; however, it worked in the end. |
| therefore | adv | /ˈðeəfɔː/ | for that reason | It was raining, and therefore the match was cancelled. |
| concept | n | /ˈkɒnsept/ | an abstract idea | The concept of freedom means different things to different people. |
| interpret | v | /ɪnˈtɜːprɪt/ | explain the meaning of | Readers may interpret the poem in many different ways. |
| hypothesis | n | /haɪˈpɒθəsɪs/ | a proposed explanation to be tested | The experiment was designed to test her hypothesis. |
| relevant | adj | /ˈreləvənt/ | connected to the matter at hand | Please include only the information that is relevant to the question. |
| define | v | /dɪˈfaɪn/ | state the exact meaning of | It is hard to define happiness in a single sentence. |
| assess | v | /əˈses/ | judge the value or quality of | Teachers assess students through both exams and projects. |
| summarise | v | /ˈsʌməraɪz/ | give a short account of the main points | Can you summarise the article in a few sentences? |
