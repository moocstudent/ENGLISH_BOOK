## Core Notes

### Past Simple

**Uses:** completed past actions (*She finished* the report); definite past time (*We moved* in 2018); past habits (*I walked* to school); sequences (*He opened, picked up and left*).

**Form:** base + **-ed** (regular) or the irregular form. Negatives and questions use **did** + base verb: ✗ Did you went? → ✓ Did you **go**?

### Past Continuous

**Uses:** action in progress at a past moment (*At 8 p.m. I was watching* TV); interrupted background (*I was cooking* when the phone rang); two parallel actions; scene-setting.

**Form:** **was / were + verb-ing** (was: I/he/she/it; were: you/we/they).

### when / while / as

| Structure | Example |
|---|---|
| when + past simple, past continuous | When she **arrived**, we **were having** dinner. |
| while / as + past continuous, past simple | While we **were having** dinner, she **arrived**. |

Long background → past continuous; short event → past simple; the event **interrupts** the background.

### Telling a story

- Set the background first (past continuous): *It was raining and I was waiting…*
- Move the main events in order (past simple): *A car stopped. A man got out.*
- Sequence words: first, then, after that, suddenly, in the end.
- Keep the main line in past simple — don't put every sentence in was + -ing.

### Irregular verbs

Learn in pattern groups: cut–cut–cut; buy–bought–bought; come–came–come; go–went–gone. Study 5–8 a day inside example sentences, starting with the highest-frequency verbs (go, have, do, get, make, take).

### Summary

Past simple = completed action + time point. Past continuous = background or interrupted action. when = short event, while/as = long background. Don't overuse was + -ing.

## Worked Examples

**Example 1.** I ___ (walk) home when it suddenly ___ (start) to rain.

> **Answer.** was walking / started

**Example 2.** Fix: *Did you went to the party?*

> **Answer.** Did you **go** to the party?

**Example 3.** Translate: 我做饭时孩子们在花园里玩。

> **Answer.** While I **was cooking**, the children **were playing** in the garden.

**Example 4.** When the teacher came in, the students ___ . (talked / were talking) → **were talking**

## Exercises

1. They ___ (arrive) at 6 a.m. → **arrived**
2. While she ___ (study), her phone ___ (ring). → **was studying / rang**
3. Fix: *I didn't knew the answer.* → **I didn't know**
4. At 9 last night we ___ dinner. (had / were having) → **were having**
5. Translate: 他小时候住在乡下。 → **He lived in the countryside as a child.**
