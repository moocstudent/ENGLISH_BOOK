## Core Notes

### Direct vs reported speech

**Direct:** the exact words in quotes — *She said, "I am tired."*
**Reported:** no quotes, often with *that* — *She said (that) she **was** tired.*

| Verb | Pattern | Example |
|---|---|---|
| say | (that) clause; say **to** sb | He said (that) he was busy. |
| tell | **sb** + (that) clause | He told **me** he was busy. |
| ask | sb to do; if / wh- clause | She asked if I was ready. |

- ✗ He said me. → ✓ He **told me** / He **said to me**.

### Backshift

When the reporting verb is **past**, tenses move back one step:

| Direct | Reported |
|---|---|
| present simple | past simple |
| present continuous | past continuous |
| present perfect / past simple | past perfect |
| will / can / may / must | would / could / might / had to |

Time words shift too: *now → then, today → that day, tomorrow → the next day, yesterday → the day before, this → that, here → there*.

### Reported questions

Change to **statement word order**; drop *do / does / did* and the question mark.

- Yes/No → **if / whether**: *"Are you ready?"* → *He asked **if I was** ready.*
- Wh- → keep the wh-word: *"Where do you live?"* → *She asked **where I lived**.*
- ✗ She asked where did I live. → ✓ …where **I lived**.

### Commands, requests, advice

Use **verb + sb + to-infinitive**; negative = **not to**.

- *"Wait."* → *He told me **to wait**.*
- *"Don't touch it."* → *He told me **not to touch** it.*
- **suggest** takes *doing* or a *that*-clause, not *sb to*: ✗ suggested me to go → ✓ **suggested going / that I go**.

### No backshift

- Reporting verb is **present / future**: *He **says** he **is** tired.*
- Still-true facts: *She said the earth **is** round.*
- *would, could, might, should* and 2nd / 3rd conditionals do not shift.

News verbs: announce, claim, report, deny, admit, warn. Passive report: *It **is reported that**… / He **is said to** be…*

### Pitfalls

- ✗ said me → told me. ✗ told to me → told me.
- ✗ suggested me to leave → suggested that I leave.
- Don't forget backshift: ✗ She said she is tired → she **was** tired.

### Summary

say + (that); tell + sb; ask + if / wh- or sb to do; a past reporting verb triggers backshift plus changed time words; reported questions use statement order; *suggest* never takes *sb to do*.

## Worked Examples

**Example 1.** *She said, "I am cooking now."* → **She said she was cooking then.**

**Example 2.** *"Where is the station?" he asked.* → **He asked where the station was.**

**Example 3.** Fix: *My teacher said me to finish it.* → **told me to finish it.**

**Example 4.** *"Don't be late," she said.* → **She told us not to be late.**

**Example 5.** Translate: 他问我第二天能不能帮他。 → **He asked me if I could help him the next day.**

## Exercises

1. Report: *"I have finished," Tom said.* → **Tom said he had finished.**
2. Report: *"Do you like jazz?" she asked.* → **She asked if I liked jazz.**
3. Fix: *He asked me what time does the shop open.* → **what time the shop opened.**
4. Report: *"Please sit down," the doctor said to him.* → **The doctor told him to sit down.**
5. Translate: 她告诉我她昨天见过他。 → **She told me she had seen him the day before.**
6. Backshift *"Water boils at 100°C"*? → **Optional — a general truth may stay *boils*.**

**Answer key.** 1 had finished · 2 if I liked jazz · 3 the shop opened · 4 told him to sit down · 5 had seen him the day before · 6 no backshift needed.
