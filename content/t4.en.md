## Core Notes

### Three ways to talk about the future

| Form | Main meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| will + base | instant decision, prediction, promise | I **will help** you. |
| be going to + base | plan, intention, evidence-based prediction | We **are going to** move. |
| present continuous | fixed arrangement (with a time) | I **am meeting** Sam at six. |

**will** = decided now (*I'll get it*); **be going to** = already planned, or there's evidence now (*Those clouds — it's going to rain*). Timetables use present simple (*The film starts at 7*).

### Modals: ability, permission, obligation, deduction

| Function | Modals | Example |
|---|---|---|
| ability | can / could | She **can** swim. |
| permission | can / may / could | **May** I come in? |
| obligation | must / have to | You **must** wear a helmet. |
| advice | should / ought to | You **should** rest. |
| possibility | may / might / could | He **might** be home. |
| deduction | must / can't | She **must** be tired. / That **can't** be true. |

Modals take the **base verb** (no -s, no to); negatives add not (can't, mustn't, shouldn't).

### have to / need to

**must** = the speaker's own emphasis; **have to** = an outside rule. The negatives differ:

| Form | Meaning |
|---|---|
| mustn't | forbidden |
| don't have to | not necessary (but allowed) |

*You mustn't park here* (forbidden) is not *You don't have to park here* (not necessary).

### Suggestions and refusals

Softer to stronger: *You could try…* / *Maybe you should…* / *You ought to…* Requests: *Could you…?* / *Would you mind…?* Polite refusal: *I'm afraid I can't…* / *I'd love to, but I have to…*

### Register

Formal writing: would, could, may, and no contractions (will not, not won't); no gonna/wanna. Informal speech relaxes these. *Could/Would you…?* is more polite than *Can you…?*

### Summary

Future = will / be going to / present continuous. Modals take the base verb. mustn't (forbidden) is not don't have to (not necessary). Match register to context.

## Worked Examples

**Example 1.** A: These bags are heavy. B: I ___ help you. → **'ll help** (decision now); We ___ visit Rome (already booked) → **are going to visit**

**Example 2.** Fix: *You must to finish it.*

> **Answer.** You **must finish** it.

**Example 3.** Translate: 你不必那么早来,但绝不能迟到。

> **Answer.** You **don't have to** come so early, but you **mustn't** be late.

**Example 4.** The lights are off. They ___ be home. (can't / mustn't) → **can't**

## Exercises

1. Look at those clouds! It ___ (rain). → **'s going to rain**
2. I ___ (see) the dentist at 3 tomorrow (booked). → **am seeing**
3. Fix: *I will can come.* → **I will be able to come**
4. You ___ touch that wire. (mustn't / don't have to) → **mustn't**
5. Translate: 她一定很累。 → **She must be tired.**
