## 核心讲解

### 独立句与从句

**独立句 (independent clause):** 含主语 + 谓语,能独立表达完整意思。

- *I like coffee.*
- *The meeting ended early.*

**从句 (subordinate / dependent clause):** 也含主语 + 谓语,但由从属连词或关系词引导,**不能独立成句**。

- *… because I like coffee*(需要主句)
- *… that ended early*

**复合句 (complex sentence)** = 一个主句 + 一个或多个从句。从句按功能分三类:

| 从句类型 | 功能 | 常见引导词 | 例 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 名词从句 | 作主语/宾语/表语 | that, whether, if, what, who | I know **that** you tried. |
| 形容词从句 | 修饰名词(定语从句) | who, which, that, whose, where | the book **that** I read |
| 副词从句 | 修饰动词或全句 | because, when, although, if | I left **because** it rained. |

### 定语从句(限定与非限定)

**关系代词:** who(人,主/宾)、whom(人,宾,正式)、whose(所属)、which(物)、that(人或物,仅限定)。
**关系副词:** where(地点)、when(时间)、why(原因)。

**限定性 (defining):** 提供不可缺少的信息,**不加逗号**,可用 that。

- *The man **who** called you is my manager.*

**非限定性 (non-defining):** 补充说明,**用逗号隔开**,不用 that,关系词不可省略。

- *My manager, **who** is from Canada, called you.*

| 对比 | 限定性 | 非限定性 |
|---|---|---|
| 逗号 | 无 | 有 |
| 用 that | 可以 | 不可以 |
| 省略关系词 | 作宾语时可省 | 不可省 |

**省略关系代词:** 作**宾语**且为限定性时可省 — *the book (that) I bought*;作**主语**时不可省。

### 名词从句作主语/宾语

**that 从句:** *That he lied* surprised everyone.(主语) / I think *(that) he lied*.(宾语)
**whether / if:** I'm not sure *whether/if she will come*.(whether 更正式,且用于介词后与 to 前)
**wh- 从句:** *What she said* was true.

**关键:** 名词从句用**陈述语序**(主 + 谓),不用疑问语序。

- ✗ I don't know where **is he**. → ✓ I don't know where **he is**.

### 状语从句(时间、原因、让步)

| 意义 | 引导词 | 例 |
|---|---|---|
| 时间 | when, while, as, before, after, until, as soon as | *When* I arrive, I'll call. |
| 原因 | because, since, as | I stayed *because* I was tired. |
| 让步 | although, though, even though, while | *Although* it rained, we walked. |
| 条件 | if, unless, as long as | *Unless* you hurry, you'll be late. |
| 目的/结果 | so that, so … that | I saved money *so that* I could travel. |

**注意:** 时间与条件从句中,**用一般现在表将来**。

- ✗ When I *will arrive*, I'll call. → ✓ When I *arrive*, I'll call.

### 标点与从句位置

- 副词从句在**句首** → 后面加逗号:*Although it rained,* we walked.
- 副词从句在**句尾** → 通常不加逗号:We walked *although it rained*.
- **非限定**定语从句 → 逗号包围。
- 名词从句 → **不加**逗号。

### 常见中式错误

1. **逗号连接 (comma splice):** ✗ It was late, I went home. → ✓ It was late, **so** I went home. / It was late**;** I went home.
2. **粘连句 (run-on):** ✗ It was late I went home. → ✓ 用连词或句号分开两个句子。
3. **although…but 同现:** ✗ **Although** it rained, **but** we walked. → ✓ 删掉 but。(「虽然…但是」直译)
4. **because…so 同现:** ✗ **Because** he was tired, **so** he slept. → ✓ 删掉 so。(「因为…所以」直译)
5. **名词从句语序:** ✗ I don't know what **is this**. → ✓ I don't know what **this is**.

### 小结

- 复合句 = 主句 + 从句;三类:名词、形容词、副词。
- 定语从句:限定不加逗号(可用 that),非限定加逗号(不用 that)。
- 名词从句用陈述语序,不用疑问语序。
- although / but、because / so 不能同时出现。
- 逗号直接连两个独立句 = comma splice,需连词、分号或句号。

## 典型例句

**例 1.** 填空(用关系词):

> **题目.** This is the café ___ we first met. / She's the colleague ___ helped me.
>
> **解.** where / who — 地点用 where;人作主语用 who。

**例 2.** 改错:

> **原句.** The house which I grew up in it was small.
>
> **解.** The house **which I grew up in** was small. — 关系词已代替宾语,删掉多余的 it。

**例 3.** 翻译:

> **中文.** 虽然他很累,但他还是把报告写完了。
>
> **解.** **Although** he was tired, he **finished** the report. — 有 although 就不要再加 but。

**例 4.** 选择:

> **题目.** I don't remember ___ .
> A) where did I put it  B) where I put it  C) where put I it
>
> **解.** B — 名词从句用陈述语序,无助动词、无倒装。

**例 5.** 合并(用从句连接):

> **任务.** 合并:*The film won an award. I watched it last night.*
>
> **参考.** The film **that I watched last night** won an award.

## 练习与自测

1. 填空: The woman ___ car was stolen called the police.(关系词)
2. 改错: Because it was raining, so we stayed inside.
3. 选择: Tell me ___ .(what do you want / what you want)
4. 用非限定定语从句改写: My brother lives in Tokyo. He is a chef.
5. 找出并改正 comma splice: The shop was closed, we went home.
6. 翻译: 我不确定她是否会同意。(whether)

**参考答案.**

1) whose  2) 删掉 so:Because it was raining, we stayed inside.  3) what you want  4) My brother, **who is a chef**, lives in Tokyo.  5) …closed, **so** we went home. / …closed**;** we went home.  6) I'm not sure **whether she will agree**.
