## Core Notes

### At the airport

- **Check-in:** *I'm checking in for the 10:40 to Tokyo.* / *Just this one to check, and this as carry-on.*
- **Seats:** *Could I get a window seat, if there's one left?*
- **Security:** *Do I need to take my laptop out?* / *Shoes on or off?*
- **Delays:** *Has the gate changed?* / *How long is the delay looking?*

### At the hotel

- **Check-in:** *I've got a reservation under [name].* / *Is early check-in possible?*
- **Requests:** *Any chance I could get a higher floor / a late check-out?*
- **Problems:** *The AC isn't working — could someone take a look?*

### Directions & getting around

- **Asking:** *How do I get to …?* / *Is it walkable?* / *Which line do I take?*
- **Understanding:** *take your second left*, *it's on your right*, *you can't miss it*, *ten minutes tops*
- **Taxi / ride-share:** *Could you take me to …?* / *Do you take card?* / *Keep the change.*

### Tip

Soft frames make requests sound natural: **Any chance I could…?**, **Would it be possible to…?**, **Could I get…?** — friendlier than a bare *I want…*.

## Worked Examples

**Ex 1.** Make it polite: *I want a room on a higher floor.* → **Any chance I could get a room on a higher floor?**

**Ex 2.** *Is it ___?* (can I walk there) → **walkable**

**Ex 3.** Driver: *Any bags for the ___?* (luggage space) → **trunk** (BrE: *boot*)

## Exercises

1. At the desk: *I have a reservation ___ Kim.* → **under**
2. Ask how long a trip takes: *About how long ___ it take?* → **will**
3. *Forty minutes, ___ or take.* → **give**
4. Ask if a taxi accepts cards. → *e.g.* **Do you take card?**
