## 核心讲解

### 入座与开场

- *A table for two, please.*(两位)/ *Do you have anything available now, or is there a wait?*(现在有位吗,还是要等)
- 服务员开场: *Can I start you off with something to drink?*(先来点喝的吗)/ *Are you ready to order?*(可以点了吗)
- 还没想好: *Could we get a couple more minutes?*(再给我们几分钟)

### 点单

- **最常用句:** *I'll have the …*(我要……)/ *Can I get the …?*
- **问菜品:** *What comes with it?*(配什么)/ *Is it spicy?*(辣吗)/ *Is the … any good?*(……好吃吗)
- **问推荐:** *What do you recommend?* / *What's popular here?*(招牌是什么)

### 定制与忌口

- *Can I get the dressing **on the side**?*(酱汁单独放)
- *Could you make it **without** onions?*(不要洋葱)/ *Is it possible to swap the fries **for** a salad?*(薯条换沙拉)
- *I have a **nut allergy** — does this have nuts in it?*(我对坚果过敏,这个有坚果吗)

### 堂食、外带与买单

- *For here*(堂食)vs. *to go*(外带)—— *Can I get that **to go**?*
- *Could we get the **check**(美)/ **bill**(英), please?*(买单)
- *Are you paying together or separately?*(一起付还是分开)→ *We'll **split it**.*(我们 AA)/ *It's on me.*(我请)

### 出问题时

保持礼貌、间接: *Sorry, I think there's been a **mix-up** — I ordered the chicken.*(好像上错了,我点的是鸡肉)服务员会 *get it sorted*(帮你处理好)。

## 典型例句

**例 1.** 酱汁单独放: *Can I get the dressing ___ ___ ___?* → **on the side**

**例 2.** 要外带: *Can I get that ___ ___?* → **to go**

**例 3.** 要账单(美式): *Could we get the ___?* → **check**

## 练习与自测

1. 礼貌点单: *I ___ have the grilled salmon.* → **'ll**
2. 不要洋葱: *Could you make it ___ onions?* → **without**
3. AA 买单: *We'll ___ it.* → **split**
4. 上错菜: *I think there's been a ___.* → **mix-up**
