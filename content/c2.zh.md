## 原文选读 Passage

*— 改编自 Frankenstein, Mary Shelley (1818)(公版作品). Adapted from a public-domain work.*

It was a dreary night in November when my long labour came at last to its end. The rain beat against the windows, and my candle had almost burned away, when by its dying light I saw the creature open its dull yellow eye. It breathed, and a faint movement stirred its limbs.

How can I describe the horror of that moment? I had chosen his features to be beautiful. Beautiful! For nearly two years I had worked, robbing myself of rest and health, with one purpose only — to give life. And now that the work was done, the dream vanished, and a breathless dread filled my heart. The being I had made was a thing I could not bear to look upon.

I rushed from the room and hurried to my bedchamber, unable to calm my mind. At last, worn out, I fell into a troubled sleep, but my dreams were wild and full of fear. I woke in the cold, and by the pale light of the moon I saw the creature standing beside my bed. He drew back the curtain, and his eyes, if eyes they could be called, were fixed upon me. His mouth opened, and he made some low sounds, while one hand reached out as if to hold me.

I did not stay. I ran down the stairs and out into the night, and there I walked until morning, filled with the misery of what I had made.

## 参考译文 Translation

那是十一月里一个阴沉的夜晚,我漫长的劳作终于走到了尽头。雨点拍打着窗户,蜡烛也快要燃尽;就在那将熄的微光里,我看见那造物睁开了它暗黄的眼睛。它呼吸起来,四肢也微微动了一下。

我该怎样描述那一刻的恐惧呢?我本想让他生得俊美。俊美!为了赋予生命这唯一的目的,我辛劳了将近两年,连休息与健康都不顾。可如今大功告成,梦想却顿时破灭,一阵令人窒息的恐惧攫住了我的心。我亲手造出的这个存在,竟是我不忍去看的东西。

我冲出房间,奔回自己的卧室,心绪难平。终于,精疲力竭的我陷入了不安的睡眠,梦境却狂乱而可怕。我在寒冷中醒来,借着惨白的月光,看见那造物正立在我的床边。他掀开床帐,那双眼睛——如果那也能叫眼睛的话——死死地盯着我。他张开嘴,发出一些低沉的声音,一只手伸了过来,仿佛要抓住我。

我没有停留。我奔下楼梯,冲进夜色,一直走到天明,心中满是对自己所造之物的悲苦。

## 背景与理解 Notes

- **原作与作者**:《弗兰肯斯坦》(Frankenstein; or, The Modern Prometheus, 1818)是英国作家 Mary Shelley(玛丽·雪莱)的哥特/科幻经典,常被视为科幻小说的先驱。本段以维克多(Victor)第一人称讲述,改编自小说中造物苏醒的著名一幕,并非原文照录。
- **主旨**:科学家历尽艰辛"创造生命",却在梦想成真的一刻被恐惧与厌恶淹没,引出创造者的责任与代价这一核心主题。
- **结构 / 手法**:以"劳作完成—造物苏醒—惊惧逃离—夜半重现"推进;借阴雨的十一月夜、将尽的烛光、"暗黄的眼睛"等意象渲染不祥,却始终克制,不作血腥描写。
- **语气**:压抑、悔恨、恐惧交织;叙述冷静内敛,情绪却层层递进。

## 阅读理解 Reading Comprehension

1. On what kind of night does the scene take place?
- A) A dreary night in November
- B) A warm summer evening
- C) A bright winter morning
- D) A calm spring afternoon

2. How long had Victor worked to create the creature?
- A) A few days
- B) Nearly two years
- C) About six months
- D) Almost ten years

3. How does Victor feel once the creature comes to life?
- A) Proud and joyful
- B) Curious and calm
- C) Bored and tired
- D) Horrified and full of dread

4. In "a breathless dread filled my heart," the word *dread* most nearly means
- A) mild surprise
- B) deep joy
- C) great fear
- D) quiet hope

5. What does Victor do at the end of the passage?
- A) He speaks kindly to the creature.
- B) He runs away into the night.
- C) He destroys his laboratory.
- D) He falls peacefully asleep.

**答案与解析 Answer Key**

1) **A** — 细节题。开篇即点明"a dreary night in November"(十一月一个阴沉的夜晚),故选 B。
2) **B** — 细节题。原文:"For nearly two years I had worked"(我辛劳了将近两年),故选 C。
3) **D** — 推断·主旨题。造物睁眼后,他感到"the horror of that moment""a breathless dread filled my heart",且"could not bear to look upon"其造物,可见充满恐惧与厌恶,故选 C。
4) **C** — 词义题。*dread* 意为强烈的恐惧;上下文的 horror(惊恐)与其后夺门而逃的举动都印证这一点,故选 A。
5) **B** — 手法·情节题。结尾他"ran down the stairs and out into the night"(奔下楼梯,冲进夜色),即逃入夜中,故选 C。
