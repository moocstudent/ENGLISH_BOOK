## 核心讲解

### 直接引语 vs 间接引语

**直接引语 (direct speech):** 引号内原封不动的话。

- *She said, "I am tired."*

**间接引语 (reported speech):** 转述话语,不加引号,常用 that 连接。

- *She said (that) she **was** tired.*

**say / tell / ask 的区别:**

| 动词 | 后接 | 例 |
|---|---|---|
| say | (that) 从句;say **to** sb | He **said** (that) he was busy. |
| tell | **sb** + (that) 从句 | He **told me** (that) he was busy. |
| ask | sb + to do;if / wh- 从句 | She **asked** if I was ready. |

- ✗ He *said me* the news. → ✓ He **told me** the news. / He **said to me** the news.

### 时态后退规则

当**转述动词为过去时**(said, told…),从句时态整体**后退一级**:

| 直接引语 | 间接引语 |
|---|---|
| 一般现在 | 一般过去 |
| 现在进行 | 过去进行 |
| 现在完成 | 过去完成 |
| 一般过去 | 过去完成 |
| will | would |
| can | could |
| may | might |
| must | had to |

**时间与指示词也随之变化:**

| 直接 | 间接 |
|---|---|
| now | then |
| today | that day |
| tomorrow | the next day |
| yesterday | the day before |
| this | that |
| here | there |
| ago | before |

- *"I saw her **yesterday**."* → *He said he **had seen** her **the day before**.*

### 转述疑问(语序变化)

转述疑问句时,一律改为**陈述语序**,去掉问号和助动词 do / does / did。

**一般疑问句:** 用 **if / whether** 引导。

- *"Are you ready?"* → *He asked **if I was** ready.*

**特殊疑问句:** 保留 **wh- 词** + 陈述语序。

- *"Where do you live?"* → *She asked **where I lived**.*
- ✗ She asked where *did I live*. → ✓ She asked where **I lived**.

### 转述建议、命令、请求

用 **动词 + sb + to 不定式** 转述命令与请求;否定用 **not to**。

| 功能 | 转述动词 | 例 |
|---|---|---|
| 命令 | tell / order sb to | He **told me to** wait. |
| 请求 | ask sb to | She **asked me to** help. |
| 建议 | advise sb to;suggest doing / that | He **advised me to** rest. |
| 提醒/警告 | remind / warn sb (not) to | She **warned me not to** go. |

- *"Don't touch it."* → *He told me **not to touch** it.*
- **suggest 特例:** ✗ He *suggested me to* leave. → ✓ He **suggested (that) I leave** / **suggested leaving**。

### 不必后退的例外 与 新闻引述

**无需后退的情况:**

- 转述动词为**现在或将来**时:*He **says** he **is** tired.*
- 陈述**至今仍然成立**的事实或真理:*She said the earth **is** round.*(可不后退)
- **过去完成、would / could / might / should、第二、三条件句**本身不再后退。

**新闻报道常用引述动词:** announce, claim, report, add, insist, deny, admit, warn, reveal。

- *The minister **denied that** he had lied.*
- **被动引述:** *It **is reported that**… / He **is said to** be…*

### 常见中式错误

1. ✗ He *said me*. → ✓ He **told me** / He **said to me**。
2. ✗ She asked me *where did I go*. → ✓ …where **I went**。
3. ✗ He *suggested me to* leave. → ✓ He **suggested that I leave**。
4. ✗ He *told to me* that… → ✓ He **told me** that…
5. **漏后退:** ✗ She said she *is* tired. → ✓ She said she **was** tired.

### 小结

- say + (that);tell + sb;ask + if / wh- 或 sb to do。
- 转述动词为过去 → 时态后退一级,时间词随之变化。
- 转述疑问用陈述语序,去掉 do / does / did 与问号。
- 命令与请求 → tell / ask sb (not) to do;suggest 不接 sb to do。
- 转述动词为现在,或内容仍成立时,可不后退。

## 典型例句

**例 1.** 变间接引语:

> **原句.** She said, "I am cooking dinner now."
>
> **解.** She said (that) she **was cooking** dinner **then**. — 现在进行→过去进行,now→then。

**例 2.** 转述疑问:

> **原句.** "Where is the station?" he asked.
>
> **解.** He asked **where the station was**. — wh- 词 + 陈述语序,去掉问号。

**例 3.** 改错:

> **原句.** My teacher said me to finish the essay.
>
> **解.** My teacher **told me to** finish the essay. — say 不直接接人做宾语;命令用 tell sb to。

**例 4.** 转述命令(否定):

> **任务.** 转述:*"Don't be late," she said to us.*
>
> **参考.** She told us **not to be** late.

**例 5.** 翻译:

> **中文.** 他问我第二天能不能帮他。
>
> **解.** He asked me **whether/if I could help him the next day**. — 一般疑问用 if/whether;can→could;tomorrow→the next day。

## 练习与自测

1. 变间接: "I have finished my homework," Tom said.
2. 转述疑问: "Do you like jazz?" she asked me.
3. 改错: He asked me what time does the shop open.
4. 转述命令: "Please sit down," the doctor said to him.
5. 翻译: 她告诉我她昨天见过他。
6. 判断: 转述 "Water boils at 100°C" 时,boils 是否必须改为 boiled?

**参考答案.**

1) Tom said (that) he **had finished** his homework.  2) She asked me **if/whether I liked** jazz.  3) …what time **the shop opened**.  4) The doctor **told him to** sit down.  5) She told me (that) she **had seen** him **the day before**.  6) 不必;这是普遍真理,可保留 boils(改为 boiled 也可接受)。
