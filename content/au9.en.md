## Transcript

Good morning, and welcome to your weather update for the whole country.

Today starts bright and sunny across most of the south, with temperatures climbing to around 24 degrees by the afternoon.

In the north, however, it will be much cooler, with cloud, a light breeze, and highs of just 15 degrees.

There is a warning of strong winds along the south coast this evening, so please take care if you are driving.

Heavy rain is expected to reach the north-west overnight, and it could last until dawn.

Tomorrow morning will feel colder everywhere, dropping to around 8 degrees first thing.

The rain should clear by midday, leaving bright spells and the odd shower in the afternoon.

Winds will ease off tomorrow, so it will feel calmer and more pleasant.

If you are heading out today, remember to take an umbrella and wrap up warm in the north.

That is all from me — have a lovely day, and stay safe out there.

## Notes

- **Genre:** a friendly TV/radio-style weather forecast (a monologue), in fairly full sentences.
- **Key info to catch:** today's weather and temperatures in the south and the north, the warnings (where the strong winds and the heavy rain are), tomorrow's changes, and the advice.
- **Listening tip:** numbers and temperatures (24°C / 15°C / 8°C), place words (south / north / south coast / north-west) and set forecast phrases (highs, bright spells, ease off) are common test points.
