## 原文选读 Passage

*— 改编自 Narrative of the Life of Frederick Douglass, Frederick Douglass (1845)(公版作品). Adapted from a public-domain work.*

When I first came to live in Baltimore, my new mistress was a kind woman who had never owned a slave before. She did not yet know the rules of slavery. Seeing that I wanted to learn, she began to teach me the letters of the alphabet, and soon I could spell short words. I felt proud, and she seemed pleased with what I had done.

But when her husband found out, he was angry and told her to stop at once. "Teach him to read," he warned her, "and he will no longer be fit to be a slave." Learning, he said, would spoil me, make me discontented, and put thoughts of freedom into my head. A slave, he believed, should know nothing but the orders of his master.

His words were meant to keep me down, yet they had the opposite effect. For the first time I saw the truth clearly. The very thing my master feared was the very thing I most needed. Reading was the road that led from slavery toward freedom. If knowledge was so dangerous to him, it must be precious to me.

From that day I made a silent promise. Whatever it might cost, I would learn to read. My mistress could teach me no more, and in time slavery hardened her too. So I found other teachers — poor white boys I met in the street — and, letter by letter, I began the long journey toward freedom.

## 参考译文 Translation

我初到巴尔的摩生活时,新的女主人是位善良的妇人,以前从未拥有过奴隶。她还不懂得奴隶制的种种规矩。见我想学,她便开始教我认字母,不久我就能拼出一些短小的单词。我心里很自豪,她似乎也为我学会的东西而高兴。

可是,当她的丈夫发现此事,他勃然大怒,叫她立刻停手。"你教会他读书,"他警告她说,"他就再也不适合当奴隶了。"他说,读书识字会把我惯坏,使我心生不满,还会让我脑子里装进自由的念头。他认为,奴隶除了主人的命令,什么都不该知道。

他这番话本想压制我,却适得其反。我第一次把真相看得清清楚楚:主人最害怕的东西,正是我最需要的东西。读书,正是那条从奴役通往自由的道路。既然知识对他如此危险,它对我就必定十分珍贵。

从那天起,我暗自立下誓言:无论付出什么代价,我都要学会读书。女主人再也不能教我了,而奴隶制日久也使她变得冷酷。于是我另寻老师——就是我在街头结识的那些贫穷的白人男孩——一个字母一个字母地,我踏上了通往自由的漫漫长路。

## 背景与理解 Notes

- **原作与作者**:《弗雷德里克·道格拉斯自述》(1845)是美国废奴运动领袖、曾为奴隶的 Frederick Douglass(弗雷德里克·道格拉斯)所写的自传,以第一人称回顾其为奴与追求自由的经历。本段为面向学习者的改写,并非原文照录。
- **主旨**:识字是奴隶挣脱枷锁、走向自由的关键;主人越是禁止,越反证了知识的力量。
- **结构 / 手法**:按"开始学字母—主人禁止—幡然醒悟—立誓自学"顺序推进;用主人的警告与"我"的领悟形成反讽对照——他最恐惧的东西,成了"我"的指路明灯。
- **语气**:回顾而坚定,由孩童天真的喜悦转为清醒的决心,充满对自由的渴望。

## 阅读理解 Reading Comprehension

1. What is the main idea of the passage?
- A) A kind mistress simply enjoyed teaching a boy to spell.
- B) Learning to read became the narrator's path toward freedom.
- C) Slaves in the city of Baltimore were treated very well.
- D) Reading was too difficult for the narrator ever to learn.

2. Who first began to teach the narrator the alphabet?
- A) His master
- B) His mistress
- C) Poor white boys in the street
- D) Another enslaved man

3. Why did the master order his wife to stop the lessons?
- A) He thought the boy was too young to learn.
- B) He wanted to teach the boy himself.
- C) He feared that an educated slave would want freedom and be hard to control.
- D) He believed reading was simply a waste of the boy's time.

4. In "he will no longer be fit to be a slave," the word *fit* most nearly means
- A) healthy
- B) suitable
- C) ready
- D) strong

5. How does the master's warning affect the narrator?
- A) It frightens him into giving up the idea of reading.
- B) It has the opposite effect and strengthens his desire to learn.
- C) It makes him feel grateful and loyal to his master.
- D) It convinces him that reading is dangerous and useless.

**答案与解析 Answer Key**

1) **B** — 主旨题。全段核心是"我"意识到 "Reading was the road that led from slavery toward freedom",并立誓自学。A、C 只是枝节且与文意不符;D 与结尾"letter by letter, I began the long journey toward freedom"相矛盾。
2) **B** — 细节题。原文明确:女主人(mistress)"began to teach me the letters of the alphabet"。街头的白人男孩是后来才找的老师(C、D 时间错位),而主人是禁止者(A)。
3) **C** — 推断题。主人警告"教他读书,他就不再适合做奴隶",并说读书会使"我"discontented、心生自由之念,可见他怕的是有知识的奴隶会追求自由、难以驾驭。
4) **B** — 词义题。"fit to be a slave"意为"适合/宜于做奴隶",fit 在此表示"合适的、胜任的",而非"健康的"或"强壮的"。
5) **B** — 语气·手法题。原文说主人的话"were meant to keep me down, yet they had the opposite effect":本欲压制,反而让"我"看清真相、更坚定地要学习。
