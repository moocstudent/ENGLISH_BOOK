## Core Notes

### Present Simple

**Uses:** habits (*I drink* coffee every morning); facts (*The earth goes* around the sun); timetables (*The train leaves* at 8:15); stative verbs (*She knows*).

**Form:** base verb; add **-s/-es** for he/she/it. Negatives: **don't / doesn't** + base. Questions: **Do/Does** + subject + base?

### Present Continuous

**Uses:** action now (*She is reading*); temporary situations (*I am staying* with my aunt); trends; fixed near future (*We are meeting* tomorrow).

**Form:** **am / is / are + verb-ing**

### Choosing between them

| Signals | Tense |
|---|---|
| every day, usually, always | present simple |
| now, at the moment, currently | present continuous |
| this week, these days | often continuous (temporary) |

### Stative verbs

Usually **no** progressive: know, believe, love, hate, want, need, seem, belong, remember, understand.

- ✗ I am knowing → ✓ I know

### Summary

Signal words pick the tense — don't translate word-for-word from Chinese.

## Worked Examples

**Example 1.** She usually ___ (walk) to work, but today she ___ (take) the bus.

> **Answer.** walks / is taking

**Example 2.** Fix: *I am thinking you are right.*

> **Answer.** I **think** you are right.

**Example 3.** Look! The children ___ in the garden. → **are playing**

## Exercises

1. Water ___ (boil) at 100°C. → **boils**
2. Listen! Someone ___ (knock). → **is knocking**
3. Fix: *She is wanting a laptop.* → **She wants**
4. Write one habit sentence + one “right now” sentence.
5. I ___ to the gym twice a week. → **go**

**Answer key.** See items 1–3 and 5 above; (4) self-check tenses.
