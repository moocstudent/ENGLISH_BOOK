## 写作任务 Task

就一个话题写一个正反观点段落(如「智能手机的利与弊」,也可选在线学习、城市生活 vs 乡村生活):先用主题句点明话题,给出至少一个优点(配连接词),再用转折词转到至少一个缺点,最后用一句带自己观点的话做平衡总结。

*(70–120 词)*

## 评分要点 Success Criteria

- 主题句点明话题,读起来平衡、不一开始就偏一边
- 优点、缺点各至少一个,并用连接词衔接
- 用转折词(however / on the other hand)清晰转到另一面
- 结尾给出自己的观点,而不只是罗列两面

## 有用句型 Useful Phrases

- Smartphones have become a part of everyday life, and they bring both benefits and drawbacks.
- On the one hand, one clear advantage is that they let us stay connected wherever we are.
- For example, we can message family, check maps and pay bills in just a few seconds.
- On the other hand, a serious drawback is that they can be addictive and distracting.
- However, spending too long on a screen can harm our sleep and concentration.
- Overall, in my view the benefits outweigh the problems, as long as we use them sensibly.

## 参考范文 Model Answer

> Smartphones have quickly become part of daily life, and they bring both clear benefits and real drawbacks. On the one hand, one obvious advantage is convenience: they let us message friends, find directions and pay for things in seconds, wherever we are. On the other hand, a serious drawback is that they can be very distracting. Many people, for example, check their phones constantly and sleep badly because of them. Overall, in my view the advantages outweigh the disadvantages, but only if we set limits and put the phone down sometimes.

*一个正反观点段落:主题句点明话题 →(on the one hand)给出一个优点并举例 →(on the other hand / however)转到一个缺点 → 结尾用 overall / in my view 给出自己的平衡结论。*

## 提示 Notes

- **结构**:主题句 → 优点(one advantage / on the one hand)→ 缺点(however / on the other hand / a drawback)→ 结论(overall / in my view)。
- **连接词**:两面之间一定要有转折词把利弊清楚分开;每一面最好配一个理由或例子(because / for example)。
- **即时反馈**:文本框上方作答,点「检查反馈」查字数、要点覆盖与常见问题;再点「看范文」对照平衡的结尾观点。
