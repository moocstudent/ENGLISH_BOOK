## 核心讲解

### 面试流程与语言

一场面试通常分五段,每段有对应的英文表达:

1. **寒暄(small talk):** *Thanks for having me.* / *It's great to finally meet you.*
2. **自我介绍:** 见下节的「现在—过去—未来」框架。
3. **行为面试题(behavioral):** 用 STAR 结构回答(见后)。
4. **你的提问(反问):** *I do have a couple of questions, if that's okay.*
5. **收尾:** *Thanks for your time — I'm really excited about this role.*

**自我介绍两种时长:**

- **1 分钟版:** 现在做什么 + 一项相关亮点 + 为何来应聘。
- **3 分钟版:** 再加入 1–2 个具体成果与一段职业动机。

### 常见问题的英文框架

| 问题 | 回答框架 |
|---|---|
| Tell me about yourself. | 现在 → 过去 → 未来(为何是这份工作) |
| Why this role or company? | 认同点 + 我能带来什么 |
| What are your strengths? | 优势 + 一个具体例子 |
| Tell me about a time… | STAR |
| Salary expectations? | 给区间 + 表示可谈 |

**STAR 骨架(行为题必备):**

- **S – Situation:** 背景一句话。 *In my last role, our team faced…*
- **T – Task:** 你的职责。 *I was responsible for…*
- **A – Action:** 你做了什么(重点,占最多篇幅)。 *So I decided to… / I organised…*
- **R – Result:** 结果,尽量量化。 *As a result, we cut costs by 20%.*

**薪资回答模板:** *Based on my research and experience, I'm looking at somewhere between X and Y, but I'm open to discussing the whole package.*

### 弱点题怎么答

*What's your biggest weakness?* 的高分逻辑:**真实的小弱点 + 正在采取的改进**,不要用「我太追求完美」这类假谦虚。

- **框架:** 承认弱点 → 影响 → 改进措施 → 现在的进展。
- **模板:** *I used to struggle with X. It sometimes meant Y, so I started Z, and now I'm noticeably better at it.*
- **避免:** 伪装成优点的套话;致命短板(如「我不擅长按时交付」)。

### 视频面试注意点

- **设备:** 提前测试摄像头、麦克风、网络;准备备用方案(*If we get cut off, I'll dial back in.*)。
- **画面:** 摄像头与眼睛齐平,光在正前方,背景整洁。
- **眼神:** 看**镜头**而非屏幕里的脸,才显得在对视。
- **小抄:** 关键词贴在镜头旁,别整段照读。
- **准备 3 个反问:** *What does success look like in the first 90 days?* / *How would you describe the team culture?* / *What are the next steps?*

### 跟进 thank-you email

面试后 24 小时内发,简短真诚,三点即可:感谢 + 你最感兴趣的一点 + 期待下一步。

- **主题:** *Thank you — [Your Name], [Role] interview*
- **正文示例:** *Dear Ms. Lee, thank you for taking the time to meet with me today. I especially enjoyed hearing about the team's new product roadmap, and I'm excited about the chance to contribute. Please let me know if you need anything further. Best regards, Mia*

### 小结

- 自我介绍用「现在—过去—未来」;行为题用 STAR,Action 与 Result 是重点。
- 弱点题要真实 + 改进,别假谦虚,别甩致命短板。
- 视频面试看镜头、测设备、备好 3 个反问。
- 面试后 24 小时内发一封简短的 thank-you email。

## 典型例句

**例 1.** 套用 STAR 回答行为题

> **题目.** *Tell me about a time you handled a tight deadline.*
>
> **参考.** *(S) Last quarter, a client moved their launch up by two weeks. (T) I had to deliver the report early without cutting quality. (A) I re-planned the tasks, dropped two low-value items, and asked a teammate to cover testing. (R) We shipped on time, and the client renewed the contract.*

**例 2.** 套用 1 分钟自我介绍框架

> **任务.** 填出「现在—过去—未来」三句。
>
> **参考.** *I'm a marketing specialist with four years in e-commerce. Before that, I studied communications and ran a student blog that reached 10,000 readers. Now I'm looking to grow into a content-strategy role, which is exactly why this position caught my eye.*

**例 3.** 弱点题应答

> **题目.** *What's your biggest weakness?*
>
> **参考.** *I used to take on too much myself instead of delegating, and it slowed the team down. So I started sharing tasks and setting clear check-ins, and my last project finished a week early.*

**例 4.** 改错(自夸过头与语法)

> **原句.** *I am the best worker and never make mistake.*
>
> **解.** *I'm a reliable worker, and I learn quickly from mistakes.* —— 避免绝对化自夸;mistake 应为复数 mistakes。

**例 5.** thank-you email 片段

> **任务.** 写感谢邮件的开头两句。
>
> **参考.** *Thank you for the great conversation this morning. I left even more excited about the role, especially the focus on customer research.*

## 练习与自测

1. STAR 分别代表哪四个词?
2. 自我介绍推荐用哪三段式结构?
3. 改错: *My weakness is I am too perfect.*
4. 视频面试时应看哪里才像在对视?
5. 写一个面试收尾时的 thoughtful 反问。
6. thank-you email 一般应在面试后多久内发出?

**参考答案.**

1) Situation, Task, Action, Result。 2) 现在—过去—未来(Present–Past–Future)。 3) 例:*I sometimes focus too much on details, so I now set time limits for each task.*(避免假谦虚)。 4) 看摄像头镜头。 5) 例:*What does success look like in the first 90 days?* 6) 24 小时内。
