## Speak & Score

> 🎙️ Practise line by line in the **interactive practice** above: press **🔊 Listen** for the model, then **🎤 Speak** to read it aloud — the browser recognises what you say and scores the match, listing any missed words. Needs mic permission; use Chrome, Edge or Safari.

## Key Phrases

- **Thank you for taking the time to meet me today.**
  *💡 Warm and unhurried; stress 'TIME'.*
- **I have three years of experience in digital marketing.**
  *💡 Link 'years of'; stress 'ex-PE-rience'.*
- **I'd describe myself as organised, calm and reliable.**
  *💡 Pause briefly between the three adjectives.*
- **My biggest strength is staying calm under pressure.**
  *💡 Finish 'strength' clearly; stress 'CALM'.*
- **I work well with others and share ideas openly.**
  *💡 Link 'work well'; 'openly' has three syllables.*
- **I'm a quick learner and I enjoy new challenges.**
  *💡 Crisp /k/ sounds in 'quick learner'.*
- **I'm looking for a role where I can grow.**
  *💡 Lift the pitch on 'grow' to sound motivated.*
- **Could you tell me more about this role?**
  *💡 Let the pitch rise a little at the end.*
- **I'm confident I can add real value here.**
  *💡 Stress 'CON-fident'; don't rush 'value'.*
- **When would you like me to start?**
  *💡 A polite question — a slight rise at the end.*
- **Thank you again for your time today.**
  *💡 Sincere close; let your voice fall at the end.*

## Notes

- **How to practise**: press '🔊 Listen' for the tone first, then '🎤 Speak' and read the line aloud; your score is how well the recognised words match the target, so speak clearly and don't rush.
- **Where you'll use it**: formal interviews, phone or video screening calls, HR questions, and the questions you ask at the end.
- **Tip**: keep a calm, confident tone — let the pitch settle at the end and don't rush the lines about your strengths; recognition needs mic permission, so use Chrome, Edge or Safari.
