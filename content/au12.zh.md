## 听力原文 Transcript

Good morning, and welcome to the nine o'clock news update here on Fernbrook Community Radio.
*早上好,欢迎收听 Fernbrook 社区电台的九点新闻简讯。*

In local news, the town's brand-new public library will open its doors on Saturday the fifteenth of March.
*本地新闻方面,本镇全新的公共图书馆将于 3 月 15 日星期六正式开放。*

The mayor will cut the ribbon at ten in the morning, and there will be free story-time sessions for children all day.
*市长将在上午十点剪彩,当天全天还会为孩子们举办免费的故事会。*

Drivers should be aware that Bridge Street will be closed for repairs from Monday until Thursday next week.
*请各位司机注意,Bridge Street 将于下周一至周四因维修封闭。*

The council is asking everyone to use Mill Lane instead, and to leave a few extra minutes for the journey.
*镇政府请大家改走 Mill Lane,并为路程多留出几分钟。*

And finally, this weekend sees the return of the Fernbrook Fun Run, in aid of the local animal shelter.
*最后一条,本周末 Fernbrook 趣味跑将再度举行,为本地动物收容所募捐。*

Runners of all ages are welcome, and the five-kilometre route starts at Riverside Park at nine on Sunday morning.
*欢迎各年龄段的跑者参加,5 公里路线于周日上午九点从 Riverside Park 出发。*

You can still sign up on the day for a small fee of five pounds, and every penny goes to help the shelter.
*当天仍可现场报名,费用仅 5 英镑,所得款项将全数用于帮助收容所。*

Now, over to the weather: it will be cloudy this morning, with sunshine and light winds expected by the afternoon.
*接下来是天气:今天上午多云,预计下午转晴、微风。*

That's all for now — stay tuned for more updates at midday.
*今天的新闻就到这里——请继续锁定,中午还有更多更新。*

## 背景与理解 Notes

- **体裁**:本地电台新闻简报(独白),正式播报语气,信息密集。
- **要点信息**:每条新闻抓「事件 + 日期/时间 + 地点」;注意 in local news / and finally 等分段信号词。
- **听力策略**:日期(15 March)、时间(ten in the morning / nine on Sunday)、地点(Bridge Street、Riverside Park)是常考点。
