## 原文选读 Passage

*— 改编自 The Time Machine, H. G. Wells (1895)(公版作品). Adapted from a public-domain work.*

The Time Traveller leaned back in his chair that evening and looked round at us with a slow smile. "You must follow me carefully," he said. "I shall have to argue against one or two ideas that almost everyone accepts as true."

He told us that a line has length only; a square has length and breadth; a cube has length, breadth, and height. But no cube, he said, can truly exist unless it also lasts through time. Time, he insisted, is simply a fourth dimension, no different from the three we know — except that our minds move along it, from the cradle to the grave, and cannot turn back.

We listened, half amused and half doubtful. Then he rose, left the room, and returned with a small machine of shining metal, ivory, and clear crystal, no larger than a little clock. He set it on a table beneath the lamp and asked one of us, the Psychologist, to press a small lever.

There was a breath of wind; the lamp flame bent. The little machine grew suddenly dim, turned for a moment like a ghost of brass and ivory, and then — it was gone. Nothing remained but the empty table.

For a moment none of us spoke. Then everyone began at once. Where had it gone? Was it a trick of mirrors? The Time Traveller only watched our astonished faces, quietly enjoying the confusion he had caused.

## 参考译文 Translation

那天晚上,时间旅行者向后靠在椅背上,带着缓缓的微笑环视我们。"你们必须仔细听我说,"他道,"我将要反驳一两个几乎人人都当作真理的观念。"

他说,一条线只有长度;一个正方形有长度和宽度;一个立方体则有长度、宽度和高度。可他又说,任何立方体若不能在时间中持续存在,就不可能真正存在。他坚称,时间不过是第四个维度,与我们所知的三个维度并无不同——只是我们的意识沿着它移动,从生到死,无法回头。

我们听着,半觉有趣,半存怀疑。随后他起身离开房间,又拿着一台小小的机器回来;那机器由闪亮的金属、象牙和透明的水晶制成,不比一只小钟大。他把它放在灯下的桌上,请我们当中的那位心理学家去按一根小小的操纵杆。

一阵微风拂过,灯焰随之一弯。那小机器骤然变暗,像一团黄铜与象牙的幻影般旋转了一瞬,然后——便消失了。桌上空空如也,什么也没剩下。

有那么一刻,谁也没有出声。接着,所有人同时开口。它去了哪里?是镜子的把戏吗?时间旅行者只是望着我们惊愕的面孔,静静地享受着他所引起的这场慌乱。

## 背景与理解 Notes

- **原作与作者**:《时间机器》(The Time Machine, 1895)是英国作家 H. G. Wells(赫伯特·乔治·威尔斯)的中篇小说,被视为现代科幻的奠基之作,"时间旅行"设定影响深远。本段以在场客人第一人称叙述,改编自小说开头晚宴上的一幕,并非原文照录。
- **主旨**:时间旅行者提出"时间是第四维度"的观点,并用小模型的凭空消失,把抽象理论化为惊人一幕,制造悬念。
- **结构 / 手法**:先以几何类比展开论证(线—面—体—时间),再以实物演示(小模型)收束;由"怀疑—演示—震惊"层层推进,理论与戏剧性相结合。
- **语气**:从容、略带神秘;时间旅行者自信而狡黠,客人则由半信半疑转为惊愕。

## 阅读理解 Reading Comprehension

1. What is the Time Traveller's main argument?
- A) That time can be stopped with a machine
- B) That geometry has only two dimensions
- C) That time is a fourth dimension, like length, breadth, and height
- D) That the human mind cannot understand time

2. What did the Time Traveller place on the table beneath the lamp?
- A) A large brass engine
- B) A small model of metal, ivory, and crystal
- C) An old wooden clock
- D) A glass ball

3. In "our minds move along it, from the cradle to the grave," the phrase *from the cradle to the grave* means
- A) from morning until night
- B) from spring to winter
- C) from home to work
- D) from birth until death

4. When the Psychologist pressed the lever, the model machine
- A) grew larger and heavier
- B) rang loudly like a bell
- C) faded and then disappeared
- D) rolled onto the floor

5. The guests' reaction at the end shows that they were
- A) amazed and full of questions
- B) calm and unsurprised
- C) angry and ready to leave
- D) certain the machine was broken

**答案与解析 Answer Key**

1) **C** — 主旨题。时间旅行者由线、面、体的维度类比,得出"Time... is simply a fourth dimension"(时间不过是第四维度),故选 B。
2) **B** — 细节题。原文:一台"small machine of shining metal, ivory, and clear crystal"(金属、象牙与水晶制成的小机器),放在灯下桌上,故选 B。
3) **D** — 词义题。*from the cradle to the grave* 字面为"从摇篮到坟墓",即从出生到死亡、贯穿一生,故选 B。
4) **C** — 细节·推断题。按下操纵杆后,机器"grew suddenly dim""then — it was gone"(骤然变暗,随即消失),故选 C。
5) **A** — 语气·手法题。结尾众人先"none of us spoke",随即"everyone began at once"追问它去了哪里,而时间旅行者望着他们"astonished faces",可见他们惊愕不已、议论纷纷,故选 B。
