> 🎙️ Practise line by line in the **interactive practice** above: press **🔊 Listen** for the model, then **🎤 Speak** to read it aloud — the browser recognises what you say and scores the match, listing any missed words. Needs mic permission; use Chrome, Edge or Safari.

## Key Phrases

- **Could I get a medium latte, please?**
  *💡 Link 'Could I' → /kʊdaɪ/; 'latte' is /ˈlɑːteɪ/.*
- **Do you have this in a medium?**
  *💡 Let 'medium' rise at the end — it's a question.*
- **Can I pay by card?**
  *💡 Glide from 'pay' to 'by' with no pause.*
- **Could I get the bill, please?**
  *💡 US usually says 'check'; UK says 'bill'.*
- **I'd like a table for two, please.**
  *💡 Say 'I'd like' quickly → /aɪdlaɪk/.*
- **Excuse me, do you have this in another colour?**
  *💡 Stress 'exCUSE me'.*
- **Sorry, where can I find the milk?**
  *💡 'can I' reduces to /kənaɪ/.*
- **Could I get this to go, please?**
  *💡 'to go' = takeaway (the UK term).*
- **I'd like to return this. Do I need the receipt?**
  *💡 The 'p' in 'receipt' is silent: /rɪˈsiːt/.*
- **Is this on sale, or is it full price?**
  *💡 Link 'on sale'; keep the 'l' in 'full' soft.*
- **Can I get a refund, or just an exchange?**
  *💡 The noun 'REfund' stresses the first syllable.*

## Notes

- **How to practise**: press '🔊 Listen' first, then '🎤 Speak' and read the line aloud; your score is how well the recognised words match the target, and any missed words are listed.
- **Where you'll use it**: ordering at a café, paying at a restaurant, asking for a size or colour in a shop, and returns or refunds.
- **Tip**: stay polite by opening with 'Could I…' or 'I'd like…' and ending with 'please'; mind the silent 'p' in 'receipt'.
