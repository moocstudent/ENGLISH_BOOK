## 原文选读 Passage

*— 改编自 Little Women, Louisa May Alcott (1868)(公版作品). Adapted from a public-domain work.*

"Christmas won't be Christmas without any presents," grumbled Jo, stretched out on the rug in front of the fire. Meg sighed and said it was simply horrid to be poor, looking down at her worn old dress. Amy gave a little sniff and complained that it was unfair for some girls to have so many pretty things while others had none at all. Only Beth, from her corner, said softly that they still had Father and Mother and one another, and surely that was a great deal.

For a moment the four young faces brightened in the firelight. Then they grew sober again, for Father was far away where the fighting was, and the coming winter promised to be long and cold. Their mother had gently proposed that this year no one should spend money on presents for pleasure, since so many soldiers were suffering and the family had little to spare.

Each girl owned a single dollar, and each had been quietly dreaming of how to spend it. Jo wished for a book, Meg for something pretty, Beth for new music, and Amy for a box of drawing pencils. Yet as they talked on, a warmer feeling crept into the room. It seemed selfish to buy small treats for themselves while their mother worked so hard and their father stood in danger. One by one, almost shyly, they agreed to think less of themselves and more of others.

## 参考译文 Translation

"没有礼物,圣诞节就不像圣诞节了,"乔趴在炉火前的地毯上,咕哝道。梅格叹了口气,低头看着自己那件旧裙子,说穷日子实在太难熬了。艾美轻轻抽了抽鼻子,抱怨说别的姑娘有那么多漂亮东西,自己却什么都没有,太不公平了。只有贝丝从她的角落里轻声说,她们毕竟还有爸爸和妈妈,还有彼此,这已经很难得了。

有那么一刻,四张年轻的脸在炉火映照下明亮起来。可随即又黯淡下去——因为父亲远在战火纷飞的地方,而即将到来的冬天注定又长又冷。母亲曾温和地提议,今年谁都不要为了享乐而花钱买礼物,因为有那么多士兵正在受苦,家里也实在拮据。

四个女孩每人只有一块钱,而每个人都早已在心里盘算着怎么花。乔想要一本书,梅格盼着点漂亮东西,贝丝想着新乐谱,艾美则惦记着一盒画笔。可是聊着聊着,一股暖意悄悄涌进屋里。母亲那样操劳,父亲又身处险境,自己却买些小玩意儿——这未免太自私了。于是,一个接一个,几乎有些羞怯地,她们都答应:少想着自己一点,多想想别人。

## 背景与理解 Notes

- **原作与作者**:《小妇人》(Little Women, 1868)是美国作家 Louisa May Alcott(路易莎·梅·奥尔科特)的半自传体小说,以作者与姐妹为原型,背景设在美国南北战争时期,父亲作为随军牧师远在前线。本段改编自小说开篇,并非原文照录。
- **主旨**:在清贫且父亲不在身边的圣诞前夕,四姐妹从抱怨"没有礼物"转向体谅家人、克制私心,凸显亲情与无私的主题。
- **结构 / 手法**:以对话开场,借各人的话语勾勒性格——乔率直、梅格爱美、艾美在意外表、贝丝温柔知足;情绪由"抱怨"转为"自省",一收一放。
- **语气**:温暖、家常,略带感伤;在孩子气的牢骚里,透出成长与善意。

## 阅读理解 Reading Comprehension

1. What is the main change in the sisters' feelings by the end of the passage?
- A) They decide to leave home and find work.
- B) They move from complaining about presents to wanting to be less selfish.
- C) They agree never to celebrate Christmas again.
- D) They grow angry with their mother.

2. Why did the family plan to spend no money on presents that year?
- A) The shops in the town were all closed.
- B) The sisters had already bought everything they wanted.
- C) Father was away, soldiers were suffering, and money was short.
- D) Their mother did not believe in giving gifts.

3. What does Beth's remark about Father, Mother, and each other suggest about her?
- A) She is angry that they have no money.
- B) She is grateful and values family more than gifts.
- C) She wants more presents than her sisters.
- D) She does not care about her family.

4. In "a warmer feeling crept into the room," the word *crept* most nearly means
- A) moved in slowly and quietly
- B) rushed in loudly
- C) disappeared at once
- D) was pushed away

5. The sisters' different wishes — a book, something pretty, music, and pencils — mainly help the reader to
- A) understand the price of each gift
- B) see that the girls have different characters and interests
- C) learn exactly how poor the family is
- D) guess what season it is

**答案与解析 Answer Key**

1) **B** — 主旨题。全段从乔抱怨"没有礼物"开始,到姐妹们决定"少想自己、多想别人"结束,情感由牢骚转为无私。A、C、D 均无依据。
2) **C** — 细节题。原文说父亲"far away where the fighting was",士兵们正在受苦,家里"had little to spare",所以母亲提议不花钱买礼物。
3) **B** — 推断题。贝丝说她们还有父母和彼此,"that was a great deal",说明她知足、重亲情胜过重礼物。
4) **A** — 词义题。"crept into the room"指暖意悄悄、缓缓地渗入,故 *crept* 意为"缓慢而悄无声息地进来"。
5) **B** — 语气·手法题。四人各自想要书、漂亮东西、乐谱、画笔,正是用不同的愿望来刻画不同的性格与兴趣,而非说明价格(A)或家境穷困程度(C)。
