## 原文选读 Passage

*— 改编自 The Legend of Sleepy Hollow, Washington Irving (1820)(公版作品). Adapted from a public-domain work.*

Among the wide hills beside a great river lies a small valley, one of the quietest places in the whole country. A little brook flows through it with a soft, sleepy murmur, and the loudest sound is usually the whistle of a bird or the far-off call of a crow. Years ago I once went shooting there, and I still remember the deep stillness that seemed to fill the air.

The people of the valley say that a strange power hangs over the land. Some believe the place was charmed long ago, so that a dreamy spell now lies upon the minds of all who live there. They walk slowly, speak softly, and love to tell old tales. They see visions, hear music in the empty air, and often fall into long daydreams.

But the most famous story of all is that of the Headless Horseman. Long ago, they say, a soldier lost his head to a cannonball in a forgotten battle, and now his ghost rides out each night in search of it. On dark evenings he gallops along the lonely roads, his black horse breathing hard, hurrying to reach the old churchyard before morning. Travellers fear most the wooden bridge among the trees, for it is there, the people whisper, that the silent rider is most often seen.

## 参考译文 Translation

在一条大河旁那片开阔的群山之间,坐落着一个小小的山谷,是全国最幽静的地方之一。一条小溪淌过谷中,发出轻柔而困倦的潺潺声;人们通常听到的最大声响,不过是一声鸟鸣,或是远处乌鸦的啼叫。多年前我曾去那里打过猎,至今仍记得那仿佛充盈于空气之中的深深寂静。

谷里的人说,有一股奇异的力量笼罩着这片土地。有人相信,这地方很久以前就被施了魔法,于是一种梦幻般的睡意如今笼罩在所有居民的心头。他们走得很慢,说话轻声细语,喜欢讲述古老的故事;他们看见幻象,听见空中传来的音乐,还常常陷入长长的白日梦。

而所有传说中最有名的,要数无头骑士的故事。他们说,很久以前,一名士兵在一场被人遗忘的战役中被炮弹夺去了头颅,如今他的鬼魂每晚都出来寻找它。在漆黑的夜里,他沿着荒僻的道路纵马疾驰,胯下黑马喘着粗气,赶在天亮前回到那座古老的教堂墓地。旅人最怕树林间那座木桥,因为据人们私下相传,正是在那里,人们最常看见这位沉默的骑手。

## 背景与理解 Notes

- **原作与作者**:《沉睡谷传奇》(The Legend of Sleepy Hollow, 1820)出自美国作家 Washington Irving(华盛顿·欧文),是美国早期哥特与民间传说名篇,收于《见闻札记》。本段为其开篇的简化改编,并非原文照录。
- **主旨**:描写一处仿佛被"睡意魔法"笼罩的静谧山谷,并由这份梦幻般的宁静引出当地最著名的无头骑士(阵亡士兵的鬼魂)传说,营造神秘氛围。
- **结构 / 手法**:先写环境之静(小溪、鸟鸣、寂静),再写居民之"梦幻",最后引出传说,层层铺垫;以"they say""the people whisper"等口耳相传的说法,增强半信半疑的真实感与悬念。
- **语气**:悠缓、梦幻,又暗含神秘与淡淡的恐怖;叙述者带着回忆的口吻,半信半疑。

## 阅读理解 Reading Comprehension

1. What is the main quality of the valley described in the passage?
- A) Its busy markets and noisy streets
- B) Its rich farms and large towns
- C) Its cold and stormy weather
- D) Its deep quiet and dreamy stillness

2. According to the legend, how did the soldier lose his head?
- A) To a cannonball in a battle
- B) In a fall from his horse
- C) In a fire at the churchyard
- D) The story does not say

3. Why, most likely, do the people "see visions" and "fall into long daydreams"?
- A) Because a dreamy spell is said to lie over the valley
- B) Because they are often ill
- C) Because they never sleep at night
- D) Because they drink from the brook

4. In "a soft, sleepy murmur," the word *murmur* most nearly means
- A) a loud crash
- B) a low, gentle sound
- C) a bright light
- D) a sudden silence

5. Why do travellers fear the wooden bridge most of all?
- A) It is old and dangerous to cross.
- B) It is very far from the village.
- C) It is where the silent rider is most often seen.
- D) It is too narrow for a horse.

**答案与解析 Answer Key**

1) **D** — 主旨题。山谷被称作"one of the quietest places in the whole country",小溪"sleepy"、寂静"deep",且据说有"dreamy spell"笼罩。文中并未提到繁忙集市、风暴或大城镇。
2) **A** — 细节题。原文说士兵"lost his head to a cannonball in a forgotten battle"(在一场被遗忘的战役中被炮弹夺去头颅)。
3) **A** — 推断题。文本把居民梦幻般的举止归因于"dreamy spell"——它"lie upon the minds of all who live there"。其余选项均未被暗示为原因。
4) **B** — 词义题。小溪"with a soft, sleepy murmur"流过,且文中说最大的声响不过是鸟鸣,故 *murmur* 指低柔的声音,与"loud crash"相反。
5) **C** — 细节·手法题。原文说正是在这座桥上"the silent rider is most often seen",所以旅人"fear most"此处。这一细节把闹鬼固定在一个地点,增添了悬念。
