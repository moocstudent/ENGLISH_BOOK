## 核心讲解

### 一般现在时 (Present Simple)

**用法:**

- **习惯与重复:** I *drink* coffee every morning.
- **事实与真理:** The earth *goes* around the sun.
- **固定安排(日程表):** The train *leaves* at 8:15.
- **状态动词:** She *knows* the answer. (不用进行时)

**形式:** 主语 + 动词原形(第三人称单数加 -s/-es)

| 人称 | 肯定 | 否定 | 疑问 |
|---|---|---|---|
| I / you / we / they | work | don't work | Do … work? |
| he / she / it | works | doesn't work | Does … work? |

**第三人称单数规则:** play → plays; watch → watches; study → studies; go → goes

### 现在进行时 (Present Continuous)

**用法:**

- **此刻正在进行:** She *is reading* right now.
- **现阶段临时情况:** I *am staying* with my aunt this week.
- **变化趋势:** More people *are learning* English online.
- **已安排好的近期:** We *are meeting* the client tomorrow.

**形式:** am / is / are + 动词-ing

### 对比:何时用哪种?

| 信号词 | 倾向时态 |
|---|---|
| every day, usually, always | 一般现在 |
| now, at the moment, currently | 现在进行 |
| this week, these days | 现在进行(临时) |

**例句对比:**

- I *work* in finance. (长期职业 → 一般现在)
- I *am working* on a report today. (今天临时 → 现在进行)

### 状态动词(Stative Verbs)

下列动词通常**不用进行时**: know, believe, love, hate, want, need, seem, belong, contain, remember, understand, prefer

- ✗ I am knowing the answer.
- ✓ I know the answer.

**例外:** think(认为) vs think(考虑); have(拥有) vs have(吃/进行)

### 常见中式错误

1. ✗ He very likes music. → ✓ He *likes* music very much.
2. ✗ I am knowing him. → ✓ I *know* him.
3. ✗ She is work in Beijing. → ✓ She *works* in Beijing.

### 小结

- 一般现在 = 习惯、事实、日程; 第三人称加 -s。
- 现在进行 = now / 临时 / 趋势; am-is-are + -ing。
- 状态动词一般不用进行时。
- 信号词决定时态,不要中英直译。

## 典型例句

**例 1.** 用一般现在或现在进行填空:

> **题目.** She usually ___ (walk) to work, but today she ___ (take) the bus because it ___ (rain).
>
> **解.** walks / is taking / is raining
> usually → 习惯用一般现在; today / 此刻 → 现在进行; it is raining 描述当前天气。

**例 2.** 改错:

> **原句.** I am thinking you are right.
>
> **解.** I *think* you are right.
> think 表「认为」时是状态动词,不用进行时。

**例 3.** 翻译:

> **中文.** 他每周打三次网球,但现在他在开会。
>
> **解.** He *plays* tennis three times a week, but he *is in* a meeting now.

**例 4.** 选择:

> **题目.** Look! The children ___ in the garden.
> A) play  B) are playing  C) plays
>
> **解.** B) are playing — Look! 提示此刻进行。

**例 5.** 写作:

> **任务.** 用 4–5 句描述你的典型工作日 vs 今天(若不同)。
>
> **参考.** I usually wake up at 7 and check my email. I work from 9 to 6. Today I am working from home because I am not feeling well.

## 练习与自测

1. 填空: Water ___ (boil) at 100°C at sea level.
2. 填空: Listen! Someone ___ (knock) at the door.
3. 改错: She is wanting a new laptop.
4. 用现在时写一句描述你的习惯,一句描述你此刻在做什么。
5. 选择: I ___ to the gym twice a week. (go / am going / goes)
6. 翻译: 这些日子他学得很努力。

**参考答案.**

1) boils  2) is knocking  3) She *wants* a new laptop.  4) (略,自查时态)  5) go  6) He *is studying* hard these days. / These days he *is studying* very hard.
