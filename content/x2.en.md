## Core Notes

### The flow of an interview

Five stages, each with useful language:

1. **Small talk:** *Thanks for having me.* / *Great to finally meet you.*
2. **Introduce yourself:** use the Present–Past–Future frame below.
3. **Behavioral questions:** answer with STAR.
4. **Your questions:** *I do have a couple of questions, if that's okay.*
5. **Closing:** *Thanks for your time — I'm excited about this role.*

Prepare a **1-minute** intro (now + one highlight + why this job) and a **3-minute** version (add results and motivation).

### Frames for common questions

| Question | Frame |
|---|---|
| Tell me about yourself. | Now → past → future (why this job) |
| Why this role or company? | What you admire + what you bring |
| Your strengths? | Strength + one concrete example |
| Tell me about a time… | STAR |
| Salary expectations? | Give a range + stay open |

**STAR skeleton:**

- **S – Situation:** one line of context.
- **T – Task:** your responsibility.
- **A – Action:** what *you* did (most detail here).
- **R – Result:** the outcome, ideally with a number.

**Salary line:** *Based on my research and experience, I'm looking at somewhere between X and Y, but I'm open to discussing the package.*

### Answering the weakness question

Give a **real, small weakness plus how you're fixing it** — skip the fake "I'm a perfectionist."

- Frame: admit → impact → action → progress.
- Template: *I used to struggle with X. It sometimes meant Y, so I started Z, and now I'm noticeably better.*
- Avoid strengths in disguise and any deal-breaker (like missing deadlines).

### Video interviews

- **Kit:** test camera, mic, and connection; have a backup (*If we get cut off, I'll dial back in.*).
- **Frame:** camera at eye level, light in front, tidy background.
- **Eyes:** look at the **lens**, not the face on screen.
- **Notes:** keywords beside the camera, never read paragraphs aloud.
- **Ask 3 questions:** *What does success look like in the first 90 days?* / *How would you describe the team?* / *What are the next steps?*

### The thank-you email

Send within 24 hours, short and sincere: thanks + one thing you enjoyed + look forward to next steps.

- **Subject:** *Thank you — [Your Name], [Role] interview*
- **Body:** *Dear Ms. Lee, thank you for meeting with me today. I especially enjoyed hearing about the product roadmap, and I'm excited about the chance to contribute. Best regards, Mia*

### Summary

Intro = now/past/future; behavioral = STAR (focus on Action and Result); weakness = real + fixing it; video = look at the lens, test the kit, prepare 3 questions; send a short thank-you email within 24 hours.

## Worked Examples

**Example 1.** STAR for *Tell me about a time you handled a tight deadline.*

> **Answer.** *(S) A client moved their launch up two weeks. (T) I had to deliver early without cutting quality. (A) I re-planned tasks, dropped low-value items, and shared testing with a teammate. (R) We shipped on time and the client renewed.*

**Example 2.** One-minute intro.

> **Answer.** *I'm a marketing specialist with four years in e-commerce. Before that I studied communications and ran a blog that reached 10,000 readers. Now I want to move into content strategy — exactly why this role caught my eye.*

**Example 3.** Weakness answer.

> **Answer.** *I used to take on too much instead of delegating, and it slowed the team down. So I started sharing tasks with clear check-ins, and my last project finished early.*

**Example 4.** Fix (over-selling): *I am the best worker and never make mistake.*

> **Answer.** *I'm a reliable worker, and I learn quickly from **mistakes**.*

**Example 5.** Thank-you opener: *Thank you for the great conversation this morning. I left even more excited about the role, especially the focus on customer research.*

## Exercises

1. What do the letters in STAR stand for?
2. Which three-part structure suits a self-introduction?
3. Fix: *My weakness is I am too perfect.*
4. In a video interview, where should you look?
5. Write one thoughtful question to ask at the end.
6. How soon should you send a thank-you email?

**Answer key.** 1) Situation, Task, Action, Result. 2) Present–Past–Future. 3) e.g. *I sometimes focus too much on detail, so I now set time limits.* 4) The camera lens. 5) e.g. *What does success look like in the first 90 days?* 6) Within 24 hours.
