## 核心讲解

### 一般过去时 (Past Simple)

**用法:**

- **过去已完成的动作:** She *finished* the report yesterday.
- **明确的过去时间点:** We *moved* to Shanghai in 2018.
- **过去的习惯或状态:** As a child, I *walked* to school every day.
- **按顺序发生的一连串动作:** He *opened* the door, *picked up* the keys and *left*.

**形式:** 主语 + 动词过去式(规则动词加 -ed;不规则动词单独记)

| 类型 | 肯定 | 否定 | 疑问 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 规则 | worked | didn't work | Did … work? |
| 不规则 | went | didn't go | Did … go? |

**注意:** 否定与疑问用 **did** + 动词原形,主动词不再变过去式。✗ Did you went? → ✓ Did you *go*?

### 过去进行时 (Past Continuous)

**用法:**

- **过去某一时刻正在进行:** At 8 p.m. I *was watching* TV.
- **被打断的背景动作:** I *was cooking* when the phone *rang*.
- **两个同时进行的动作:** She *was reading* while he *was cooking*.
- **叙事开头渲染背景:** The rain *was falling* and the wind *was blowing*.

**形式:** was / were + 动词-ing

| 主语 | be 动词 |
|---|---|
| I / he / she / it | was |
| you / we / they | were |

### 时间连接词 when / while / as

- **when** 后多接**短暂动作**(一般过去):When the phone rang, …
- **while / as** 后多接**持续背景**(过去进行):While I was sleeping, …
- 主从句可互换位置,但时态要对应:

| 结构 | 例句 |
|---|---|
| when + 一般过去,过去进行 | When she *arrived*, we *were having* dinner. |
| while + 过去进行,一般过去 | While we *were having* dinner, she *arrived*. |

**记忆:** 「长背景」用过去进行,「短事件」用一般过去,事件**打断**背景。

### 叙述技巧与段落组织

**讲一个故事的常见时间线:**

1. **背景 (setting):** 用过去进行铺垫场景 — It *was raining* and I *was waiting* for the bus.
2. **主线事件 (events):** 用一般过去按顺序推进 — A car *stopped*. A man *got out*. He *asked* …
3. **顺序词:** first, then, after that, later, suddenly, in the end。
4. **突发:** suddenly / all of a sudden + 一般过去。

**建议:** 主线动作几乎都用一般过去;过去进行只在需要「背景」或「被打断」时使用,不要每句都 was + -ing。

### 不规则动词复习策略

**按变化模式分组记忆:**

| 模式 | 例(原形–过去–过去分词) |
|---|---|
| 三态相同 | cut–cut–cut; put–put–put |
| A-B-B | buy–bought–bought; bring–brought–brought |
| A-B-A | come–came–come; run–ran–run |
| A-B-C | go–went–gone; sing–sang–sung |

- 每天记 5–8 个,用**例句**记而非孤立单词。
- 高频动词优先:go, have, do, say, get, make, take, come, see, know。

### 常见中式错误

1. ✗ Yesterday I go to the park. → ✓ Yesterday I *went* to the park.
2. ✗ Did you saw him? → ✓ Did you *see* him?
3. ✗ When I was young, I am living in Xi'an. → ✓ … I *lived* / *was living* in Xi'an.
4. ✗ I was study when he called. → ✓ I *was studying* when he called.

### 小结

- 一般过去 = 完成的动作 + 明确时间点;规则加 -ed,不规则单记。
- 过去进行 = 过去某刻的背景或被打断的动作;was/were + -ing。
- when 接短事件,while/as 接长背景;事件打断背景。
- 叙事以一般过去为主线,过去进行只做铺垫,别滥用 was + -ing。

## 典型例句

**例 1.** 用一般过去或过去进行填空:

> **题目.** I ___ (walk) home when it suddenly ___ (start) to rain.
>
> **解.** was walking / started
> 长背景「走路」用过去进行,突发「下雨」用一般过去打断。

**例 2.** 改错:

> **原句.** Did you went to the party last night?
>
> **解.** Did you *go* to the party last night?
> 疑问句已有 did,主动词用原形 go。

**例 3.** 翻译:

> **中文.** 我做饭的时候,孩子们正在花园里玩。
>
> **解.** While I *was cooking*, the children *were playing* in the garden.

**例 4.** 选择:

> **题目.** When the teacher came in, the students ___ .
> A) talked  B) were talking  C) talk
>
> **解.** B) were talking — 老师进来(短事件)时,学生正在说话(背景)。

**例 5.** 写作:

> **任务.** 用 4–5 句讲一件昨天发生的小事,至少用一次过去进行做背景。
>
> **参考.** Yesterday morning I *was waiting* for the bus when I *saw* an old friend. We *talked* for a few minutes and then I *got* on the bus. It *made* my day.

## 练习与自测

1. 填空: They ___ (arrive) at the airport at 6 a.m.
2. 填空: While she ___ (study), her phone ___ (ring).
3. 改错: I didn't knew the answer.
4. 用 when 连接并注意时态: I / open the door. + The cat / run out.
5. 选择: At 9 o'clock last night, we ___ dinner. (had / were having / have)
6. 翻译: 他小时候住在乡下。

**参考答案.**

1) arrived  2) was studying / rang  3) I didn't *know* the answer.  4) I *was opening* the door when the cat *ran* out.  5) were having  6) He *lived* in the countryside when he was a child. / As a child, he *lived* in the countryside.
