## 写作任务 Task

给一所语言学校(或酒店)写一封简短邮件,咨询暑期英语课程的信息:先打招呼,说明写信目的,提出 2–3 个清晰的问题,请对方回复,并礼貌结尾。

*(50–95 词)*

## 评分要点 Success Criteria

- 有称呼和落款,读起来像一封询问邮件
- 开头就说清写信目的
- 提出 2–3 个具体、清晰的问题
- 语气礼貌、较为正式

## 有用句型 Useful Phrases

- I am writing to ask for some information about your summer English courses.
- I found your school online and I would like to know a few details.
- Could you tell me how much a four-week course costs?
- How long is each lesson, and how many students are there in a class?
- I would be grateful if you could reply by the end of the week.
- I look forward to hearing from you. Yours faithfully, …

## 参考范文 Model Answer

> Dear Sir or Madam,
> 
> I am writing to ask for some information about your summer English courses. I found your school online and I am interested in improving my speaking.
> 
> Could you tell me how much a four-week course costs? How many students are there in each class, and do you offer lessons in the evening?
> 
> I would be grateful for a reply by Friday. I look forward to hearing from you.
> 
> Yours faithfully,
> Ana Ruiz

*一封礼貌的询问邮件:称呼 → 说明写信目的 → 提出 2–3 个清晰问题(费用、班级人数、上课时间)→ 请求回复 → 正式结尾与落款。*

## 提示 Notes

- **结构**:称呼 → 写信目的 → 2–3 个清晰问题 → 请求回复 → 礼貌结尾 → 落款。
- **语气**:礼貌、较正式;问题要具体,一句一问,方便对方逐条回答。
- **即时反馈**:在文本框作答,点「检查反馈」查字数、任务要点覆盖与常见语言问题;再点「看范文」对照。
