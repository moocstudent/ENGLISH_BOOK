## Task

Write a complaint email to an online shop about a product that arrived faulty. Say what you bought and when, describe the problem, explain how you feel, and ask for a refund or a replacement. Keep a polite but firm tone.

*(55–100 words)*

## Success Criteria

- Has a greeting and a sign-off — it reads like a formal email
- Clearly states what was bought and what went wrong
- Expresses disappointment without being rude
- Makes a clear, specific request with a firm, polite closing

## Useful Phrases

- I am writing to complain about a product I bought from your website.
- On 5 August I ordered a coffee machine (order no. 7312).
- Unfortunately, when it arrived, the item was faulty and would not switch on.
- I am very disappointed, as this is the second time this has happened.
- I would therefore like a full refund or a replacement as soon as possible.
- I look forward to your prompt reply. Yours faithfully, …

## Model Answer

> Dear Customer Service Team,
> 
> I am writing to complain about a coffee machine I bought from your website on 5 August (order no. 7312).
> 
> Unfortunately, when it arrived, the machine was faulty: it leaks water and will not heat up. I am very disappointed, especially as delivery already took two weeks.
> 
> I would therefore like a full refund or a replacement as soon as possible. Please let me know how to return the item.
> 
> I look forward to your prompt reply.
> 
> Yours faithfully,
> Li Wei

## Notes

- **Structure**: greeting → what you bought & when → the problem → how you feel → what you want → firm polite closing → sign-off.
- **Tone**: polite but firm; state the facts and avoid insults or threats. An order number makes your case stronger.
