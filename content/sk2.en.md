## Speak & Score

> 🎙️ Practise line by line in the **interactive practice** above: press **🔊 Listen** for the model, then **🎤 Speak** to read it aloud — the browser recognises what you say and scores the match, listing any missed words. Needs mic permission; use Chrome, Edge or Safari.

## Key Phrases

- **Hey, how's it going? Good to see you.**
  *💡 Link 'how's it' → /howzit/ and keep it light.*
- **Long time no see! How have you been?**
  *💡 Even stress on all four words: LONG-TIME-NO-SEE.*
- **Did you get up to anything nice this weekend?**
  *💡 'Did you' blends into /dɪdʒə/.*
- **It's finally warming up — great weather for a walk.**
  *💡 'walk' is /wɔːk/ — the 'l' is silent.*
- **What do you like to do in your spare time?**
  *💡 'What do you' reduces to /wɒdʒə/.*
- **I'm really into photography at the moment.**
  *💡 Stress the 2nd syllable: pho-TO-graphy.*
- **Do you have any plans for the long weekend?**
  *💡 The 's' in 'plans' sounds like /z/.*
- **Anyway, I should get going. It was great catching up.**
  *💡 Link 'catching up'; a polite way to end a chat.*
- **We should grab a coffee sometime soon.**
  *💡 Link 'grab a' → /græbə/.*
- **The weather's been strange lately, hasn't it?**
  *💡 Tag question 'hasn't it?' rises at the end.*
- **How was your weekend? Did you do much?**
  *💡 Link 'was your' → /wɒʒər/.*

## Notes

- **How to practise**: press '🔊 Listen' first, then '🎤 Speak' and read the line aloud; your score is how well the recognised words match the target, and any missed words are listed.
- **Where you'll use it**: hallway hellos, chatting in the break room, breaking the ice at a party, and greeting colleagues or neighbours.
- **Tip**: small talk should sound relaxed — mind your linking and end-of-sentence intonation; a rising tag question ('hasn't it?') sounds more native.
