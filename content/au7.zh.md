## 听力原文 Transcript

**A:** Good afternoon, and welcome to the Riverside Hotel. How can I help you?
*下午好,欢迎入住河景酒店。有什么可以帮您?*

**B:** Hi, I have a reservation under the name Daniel Foster.
*你好,我有预订,名字是 Daniel Foster。*

**A:** Let me check for you... Yes, Mr. Foster, I have you down for three nights, checking out on Friday.
*我帮您查一下……有的,Foster 先生,您订了三晚,周五退房。*

**B:** That's right. I think I booked a double room.
*对。我记得订的是大床房。*

**A:** Actually, the reservation is for a twin room — that's two single beds. Shall I keep it, or change it to a double?
*其实预订上是双床房——两张单人床。要保留,还是帮您换成大床房?*

**B:** A twin is fine, thanks. Two beds actually suits us better.
*双床就行,谢谢。两张床其实更合适我们。*

**A:** Great. Could I see your passport, please? ... Thank you.
*好的。可以看一下您的护照吗?……谢谢。*

**B:** Sure. Oh, and what time is breakfast?
*给您。对了,早餐几点?*

**A:** Breakfast is served from seven to ten in the morning, in the restaurant on the ground floor.
*早餐上午七点到十点,在一楼的餐厅。*

**B:** Perfect. And is there wifi in the room?
*很好。房间里有无线网吗?*

**A:** Yes. The network is 'Riverside Guest', and the password is your room number, 412.
*有的。网络名是「Riverside Guest」,密码就是您的房号 412。*

**B:** Got it. And what time do we need to check out?
*明白了。那我们几点退房?*

**A:** Checkout is at eleven. Here's your key card — the lift is just around the corner. Enjoy your stay!
*退房时间是十一点。这是您的房卡——电梯就在拐角处。祝您入住愉快!*

**B:** Thank you very much.
*非常感谢。*

## 生词与短语 Vocabulary

| 词 / 短语 | 释义 |
|---|---|
| reservation | 预订 |
| under the name… | 以……的名字(预订) |
| twin room | 双床房(两张单人床) |
| double room | 大床房(一张双人床) |
| ground floor | 一楼(英式:底层) |
| check out | 退房;退房时间 |
| key card | 房卡 |
| lift | 电梯(英式英语) |
| Enjoy your stay! | 祝您入住愉快! |

## 背景与理解 Notes

- **体裁**:酒店前台办理入住(check-in)对话,礼貌、专业、语速平稳。
- **要点信息**:抓住「预订名字」「住几晚」「房型(twin/double)」「早餐时间+地点」「wifi 密码/房号」「退房时间」。
- **听力策略**:数字最关键——三晚、7:00–10:00、房号/密码 412、退房 11:00;注意 twin(双床)≠ double(大床)。
