## Speak & Score

> 🎙️ Practise line by line in the **interactive practice** above: press **🔊 Listen** for the model, then **🎤 Speak** to read it aloud — the browser recognises what you say and scores the match, listing any missed words. Needs mic permission; use Chrome, Edge or Safari.

## Key Phrases

- **Hello, could I speak to Mr Chen, please?**
  *💡 Link 'could I'; end with a polite falling tone.*
- **He's not here right now. Can I take a message?**
  *💡 Stress the first syllable: MES-sage.*
- **Are you free on Saturday afternoon by any chance?**
  *💡 Link 'free on'; let the pitch rise to ask.*
- **Shall we meet at the coffee shop near the station?**
  *💡 'Shall we' is a common way to suggest something.*
- **Sorry, could you hold on a moment, please?**
  *💡 Link 'hold on'; keep the tone polite.*
- **Let me check my calendar and get back to you.**
  *💡 Stress the first syllable: CA-len-dar.*
- **Does two o'clock work for you on Friday?**
  *💡 Weaken the 'o' in 'o'clock'; link 'work for'.*
- **I'm just calling to confirm our meeting tomorrow.**
  *💡 Stress the 2nd syllable: con-FIRM.*
- **Great, so that's settled. See you on Friday then!**
  *💡 'settled' is /ˈsetld/ with a light 't'; sound cheerful.*
- **Sorry to bother you. Could you speak up a little?**
  *💡 Link 'speak up'; say 'a little' lightly.*
- **Thanks so much for calling. Have a lovely day!**
  *💡 A warm way to end a call; keep the tone friendly.*

## Notes

- **How to practise**: press '🔊 Listen' first, then '🎤 Speak' and read the line aloud; your score is how well the recognised words match the target, and any missed words are listed.
- **Where you'll use it**: work calls, taking a message for a colleague, arranging a time with friends, and confirming or ending a call.
- **Tip**: when someone's unavailable use **Can I take a message? / Could you hold on?**; to fix a time use **Are you free…? / Does … work for you?**
