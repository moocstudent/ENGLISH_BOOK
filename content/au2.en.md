## Transcript

**A:** Excuse me, sorry to bother you. I think I'm a bit lost — could you tell me the way to the train station?

**B:** No problem at all. It's not far from here — about a ten-minute walk.

**A:** Oh, that's good to hear. So which way should I go?

**B:** Right. Go straight down King Street until you reach the traffic lights.

**A:** Straight down King Street, all the way to the traffic lights. Got it.

**B:** At the lights, turn left onto Queen Street. You'll walk past a big supermarket on your left.

**A:** Okay — left at the lights, then past the supermarket.

**B:** Exactly. Keep going until you come to a roundabout, and take the second exit.

**A:** The second exit at the roundabout. And then where is it?

**B:** The station is right there on your right, next to a red-brick post office. You can't miss it.

**A:** That's really helpful. Thank you so much for your help!

**B:** You're very welcome. Have a safe trip!

## Notes

- **Genre:** a street dialogue about asking for directions — natural pace, polite everyday phrases.
- **Key info to catch:** the destination, the turns (left / right), the landmarks (traffic lights, supermarket, roundabout, post office), and roughly how long the walk is.
- **Listening tips:** direction words (straight / left / right), ordinals (second exit) and place prepositions (on your right / next to) are common test points — follow the landmarks to map the route in your head.
