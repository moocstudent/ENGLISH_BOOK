## 核心讲解

### 现在完成时 (Present Perfect)

**核心:** 过去的动作与**现在**有联系,重点在**现在的结果或状态**,而非过去何时发生。

**用法:**

- **人生经验(不说具体时间):** I *have visited* Japan twice.
- **过去动作的现在结果:** She *has lost* her keys.(现在进不了门)
- **持续到现在的状态:** We *have lived* here for ten years.(现在仍住)
- **刚刚或最近完成:** I *have just finished* my homework.

**形式:** have / has + 过去分词 (p.p.)

| 主语 | 助动词 |
|---|---|
| I / you / we / they | have |
| he / she / it | has |

**信号词:** ever, never, already, yet, just, so far, recently, before。

### for / since 与时间段

- **for + 一段时间:** for two years, for a long time。
- **since + 时间起点:** since 2019, since last Monday, since I was a child。

| 表达 | 例句 |
|---|---|
| for + 时段 | I have known her *for* five years. |
| since + 起点 | I have known her *since* 2019. |

**already / yet / just 的位置:**

- **already**(已经,肯定句):I have *already* eaten.
- **yet**(还,用于否定/疑问句末):Have you finished *yet*? / Not *yet*.
- **just**(刚刚):The train has *just* left.

### 过去完成时 (Past Perfect) 与时间轴

**核心是「过去的过去」** — 在过去某时间点之前**更早**发生的动作。

**形式:** had + 过去分词。

**时间轴:**

- 更早的动作 → 过去完成 (had done)
- 较晚的动作 → 一般过去 (did)

**例:** When I *arrived* at the station, the train *had* already *left*.
(火车先走 = 过去完成;我后到 = 一般过去)

**常与:** after, before, by the time, already, when 连用。

### 与一般过去的选用

| 情况 | 用时态 | 例 |
|---|---|---|
| 具体过去时间点 | 一般过去 | I *saw* him yesterday. |
| 不提时间、重经验或结果 | 现在完成 | I *have seen* that film. |
| 过去两件事的先后 | 过去完成 + 一般过去 | She *had left* before I *called*. |

**关键:** 句中有 yesterday, last week, in 2020, ago 等**明确过去时间**时,不用现在完成,用一般过去。
✗ I have seen him yesterday. → ✓ I *saw* him yesterday.

### 英式与美式差异

- **英式**倾向:I *have* just eaten. / Have you *finished* yet?
- **美式**口语中常用一般过去:I just *ate*. / Did you *finish* yet?
- 考试写作建议遵循**英式**规则(just / already / yet 配现在完成),更稳妥。

### 常见中式错误

1. ✗ I have finished it yesterday. → ✓ I *finished* it yesterday.
2. ✗ I have lived here since three years. → ✓ … *for* three years.
3. ✗ He has gone to Paris three times. → ✓ He *has been* to Paris three times.(been = 去过并回来;gone = 去了还没回)
4. ✗ After I had ate, I left. → ✓ After I had *eaten*, I left.

### 小结

- 现在完成 = 过去动作 + 现在联系(经验/结果/持续);have/has + p.p.。
- for 接时段,since 接起点;already/just 用肯定,yet 用于否定疑问。
- 过去完成 = 过去的过去;had + p.p.,配一般过去表先后。
- 句中有明确过去时间点就用一般过去,别用现在完成。

## 典型例句

**例 1.** 用现在完成或一般过去(含过去完成)填空:

> **题目.** I ___ (never / eat) sushi before I ___ (go) to Tokyo last year.
>
> **解.** had never eaten / went
> 「去东京之前从没吃过」是过去的过去,用过去完成;last year 明确时间,用一般过去。

**例 2.** 改错:

> **原句.** She has bought a new car last week.
>
> **解.** She *bought* a new car last week.
> last week 是明确过去时间,用一般过去,不用现在完成。

**例 3.** 翻译:

> **中文.** 我认识她已经十年了。
>
> **解.** I *have known* her *for* ten years.

**例 4.** 选择:

> **题目.** By the time we got to the cinema, the film ___ .
> A) started  B) has started  C) had started
>
> **解.** C) had started — 电影先开始(过去的过去),我们后到。

**例 5.** 写作:

> **任务.** 用现在完成写 3 句你的人生经验(用 ever / never / twice 等)。
>
> **参考.** I have visited three countries. I have never tried skydiving. I have already read this book twice.

## 练习与自测

1. 填空: They ___ (live) in London since 2015.
2. 填空: ___ you ever ___ (be) to Canada?
3. 改错: I have seen this movie two days ago.
4. 用 for 或 since 填空: We have been friends ___ high school.
5. 选择: When I got home, my family ___ dinner. (already had / had already had / has already had)
6. 翻译: 火车刚刚离开。

**参考答案.**

1) have lived  2) Have … been  3) I *saw* this movie two days ago.  4) since  5) had already had  6) The train *has just left*.
