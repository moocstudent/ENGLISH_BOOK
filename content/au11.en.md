## Transcript

**A:** Good afternoon. How can I help you today?

**B:** Hi. I'd like to return this jacket, please. I bought it last week.

**A:** No problem at all. Is there something wrong with it?

**B:** It's the wrong size, actually. I need a large, but this one is a medium.

**A:** I see. Do you have the receipt with you?

**B:** Yes, here it is. I paid forty-five pounds for it.

**A:** Thank you. And have you worn it at all?

**B:** No, it still has the tags on. I only tried it on at home.

**A:** Great. We can do a refund or an exchange — whichever you prefer. Our policy is returns within thirty days.

**B:** I'd rather just have a refund, if that's okay. I found a similar one somewhere else.

**A:** Of course. Since you paid by card, the money will go back to the same card.

**B:** How long does that usually take?

**A:** Around three to five working days. I'll print you a receipt for the return.

**B:** Perfect. Thank you very much for your help.

## Notes

- **Genre:** a shop-return service dialogue — polite, transactional language.
- **Key info to catch:** the reason for the return, whether she has the receipt, refund vs exchange, the time limit (within 30 days), and how the money comes back (onto the card).
- **Listening tips:** numbers (£45, 30 days, 3–5 working days) and set phrases (within thirty days / back to the card / store credit) are common test points.
