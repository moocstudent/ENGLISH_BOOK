## Key Phrases

- **In my opinion, remote work saves everyone a lot of time.**
  *💡 Stress the 2nd syllable: o-PIN-ion.*
- **I think we should hear both sides before we decide.**
  *💡 Weaken 'should' to /ʃəd/ and say it quickly.*
- **That's a really good point — I hadn't thought of that.**
  *💡 'thought' is /θɔːt/ — mind the 'th'.*
- **I see what you mean, but I still have some doubts.**
  *💡 Pause slightly before 'but' to mark the contrast.*
- **I'm not sure I agree with that, to be honest.**
  *💡 Keep the tone soft to disagree politely.*
- **Absolutely! That's exactly how I feel about it.**
  *💡 Stress the first syllable: AB-so-lutely, with energy.*
- **I couldn't agree more with what you just said.**
  *💡 The 't' in 'couldn't' is light; this means strong agreement.*
- **Well, it depends on the situation, doesn't it?**
  *💡 Let the tag 'doesn't it?' rise at the end.*
- **What do you think about working from home?**
  *💡 Link 'think about' → /θɪŋkəˈbaʊt/.*
- **I'm afraid I have to disagree with you there.**
  *💡 'I'm afraid' softens the disagreement.*
- **You might be right, but let's look at the facts.**
  *💡 'might' shows possibility; say 'let's' quickly.*

## Notes

- **How to practise**: press '🔊 Listen' first, then '🎤 Speak' and read the line aloud; your score is how well the recognised words match the target, and any missed words are listed.
- **Where you'll use it**: meetings and discussions, friendly debates, interview answers, and showing agreement or polite disagreement.
- **Tip**: agree with **Absolutely / I couldn't agree more**; soften disagreement with **I'm afraid / I'm not sure** before giving your reason.
