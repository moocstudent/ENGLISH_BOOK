## 原文选读 Passage

*— 改编自 Pride and Prejudice, Jane Austen (1813)(公版作品). Adapted from a public-domain work.*

"It is a truth universally acknowledged, that a single man with a large fortune must be in want of a wife." Whether the young man wishes it or not, the families around him treat him at once as the rightful property of one of their daughters.

So it was in the Bennet household. One morning Mrs Bennet hurried to her husband with thrilling news: Netherfield Park, the great house nearby, had been rented at last. The new tenant, she said, was a rich young gentleman from the north of England — a Mr Bingley, single, with four or five thousand pounds a year. What a fine thing, she declared, for their five daughters!

Mr Bennet asked, calmly, how this could possibly concern the girls. His wife was astonished at him. Could he not see that Mr Bingley might marry one of them? He must therefore call on the young man the very moment he arrived.

Her husband, who dearly loved to tease her, pretended not to follow. Perhaps, he suggested, Mrs Bennet and the girls should go alone; or he might send a short letter, giving his consent to whichever daughter Mr Bingley liked best. His wife cried that he took no notice of her poor nerves. On the contrary, he replied gently, he had the greatest respect for her nerves — they had been his faithful companions for twenty years.

## 参考译文 Translation

「凡是有一大笔家产的单身男人,必定想要娶个妻子——这是一条举世公认的真理。」不管这位年轻人自己是否愿意,他周围的人家都立刻把他看作自家某个女儿理所应得的财产。

贝内特家里正是如此。一天早晨,贝内特太太急匆匆地跑来告诉丈夫一个天大的好消息:附近那座大宅子内瑟菲尔德庄园终于租出去了。她说,新房客是一位从英格兰北方来的阔气年轻绅士——姓宾利,单身,每年有四五千英镑的进项。她嚷道,这对她们家五个女儿可真是天大的好事!

贝内特先生却不慌不忙地问,这跟女儿们能有什么关系。太太对他大感惊讶。难道他看不出宾利先生可能会娶她们其中一个吗?所以,那年轻人一到,他就必须去拜访人家。

这位丈夫素来爱逗弄妻子,便假装听不明白。他提议说,或许可以让贝内特太太带着姑娘们自己去;要不然,他写封短信,准许宾利先生挑中的那个女儿嫁过去就是了。太太抱怨他一点也不体谅她那可怜的神经。他却温和地回答,恰恰相反,他对她的神经敬重得很——二十年来,它们可是他忠实的老伙伴。

## 背景与理解 Notes

- **原作与作者**:《傲慢与偏见》(Pride and Prejudice, 1813)是英国作家 Jane Austen(简·奥斯汀)最著名的小说,以细腻的社会观察和机智的对话见长。本段改编自小说开篇,并非原文照录。
- **主旨**:借那句反讽的名言,点出当时社会把「有钱的单身汉」自动看作待嫁女儿的目标,婚姻与财产紧紧相连。
- **结构 / 手法**:先以格言式的第一句立骨,再落到贝内特夫妇的具体对话;通过对话刻画人物——太太的急切与先生的冷静形成鲜明对照。
- **语气**:诙谐、反讽;叙述者看似一本正经,实则暗含调侃。

## 阅读理解 Reading Comprehension

1. The famous first sentence of the passage is best described as
- A) a serious scientific fact
- B) a sad reflection on lost love
- C) an angry complaint about rich men
- D) a playful, ironic remark about society

2. What is the exciting news that Mrs Bennet brings to her husband?
- A) One of their daughters is getting married.
- B) Mr Bennet has been left a large fortune.
- C) A wealthy single man has rented Netherfield Park.
- D) Mr Bingley has invited the family to dinner.

3. Why does Mrs Bennet want her husband to visit Mr Bingley?
- A) So that Mr Bingley might marry one of their daughters
- B) To rent Netherfield Park for themselves
- C) To borrow money from him
- D) To warn him about the neighbourhood

4. In the passage, "in want of a wife" means the man
- A) refuses to marry
- B) already has a wife
- C) needs or lacks a wife
- D) dislikes women

5. How does Mr Bennet mainly react to his wife's excitement?
- A) He shares her excitement completely.
- B) He gently teases her and pretends not to understand.
- C) He becomes angry and refuses to speak.
- D) He rushes off at once to visit Mr Bingley.

**答案与解析 Answer Key**

1) **D** — 主旨·语气题。首句把「有钱单身汉必想娶妻」当作「举世公认的真理」郑重宣布,实为反讽,是对当时社会风气的戏谑评论,而非科学事实或愤怒/伤感之词,故 其余选项均误。
2) **C** — 细节题。原文明确:内瑟菲尔德庄园「had been rented at last」,新房客是单身而阔气的年轻人宾利先生,故选 B;文中并未提及婚事已成、贝内特先生得到家产或受邀赴宴。
3) **A** — 推断题。太太惊讶丈夫看不出「Mr Bingley might marry one of them」,并要他「call on the young man the very moment he arrived」,可见她盼的是女儿能嫁给宾利。
4) **C** — 词义题。"be in want of" = 需要、缺少;结合语境,指这单身男子「需要一位妻子」,故选 B。
5) **B** — 语气·手法题。丈夫「dearly loved to tease her」,故意「pretended not to follow」,还打趣要写信让宾利挑女儿——是温和的取笑与装傻,而非附和、发怒或真的立刻去拜访。
