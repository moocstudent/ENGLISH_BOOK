## Task

Write a short email to a colleague: apologise for missing today's meeting, give a brief reason, and propose one specific new time this week.

*(45–90 words)*

## Success Criteria

- Has a greeting and a sign-off — it reads like an email
- Apologises sincerely, without over-explaining
- Offers one clear, specific new time
- Polite and concise in tone

## Useful Phrases

- I'm really sorry I missed our meeting this morning.
- Something urgent came up and I couldn't get away in time.
- I apologise for any inconvenience this may have caused.
- Could we reschedule for later this week?
- Would Thursday at 2 p.m. work for you?
- Thanks for your understanding. Best regards, …

## Model Answer

> Hi Sam,
> 
> I'm really sorry I missed our meeting this morning. A last-minute problem came up with a client and I couldn't get away in time. I apologise for any inconvenience.
> 
> Could we reschedule for later this week? Would Thursday at 2 p.m. work for you? If not, just let me know a time that suits you.
> 
> Thanks for your understanding.
> 
> Best regards,
> Wei

## Notes

- **Structure**: greeting → apology → one-line reason → propose a new time → polite closing → sign-off.
- **Tone**: sincere and concise; apologise once, don't pile up excuses.
