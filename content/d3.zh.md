## 原文选读 Passage

*— 改编自 Self-Reliance, Ralph Waldo Emerson (1841)(公版作品). Adapted from a public-domain work.*

There is a time in a person's education when he comes to believe something important: that he must trust himself. Deep in every heart there is a private voice, a thought that belongs to no one else. Learn to listen to it. The great people we admire were not great because they copied others; they were great because they believed their own thoughts and dared to speak them aloud.

Envy, I think, is only a form of ignorance, and to imitate another person is to slowly destroy yourself. When you try to be someone else, you throw away the one gift that is truly your own. Nature gave each of us a particular place and a particular task, and no one else can do exactly what you were born to do. If you spend your whole life copying your neighbour, the world never receives the thing that only you could give.

The hardest part is that society does not want you to think for yourself. It would rather that everyone agreed, followed the same customs, and shared the same opinions. To keep its good opinion, most people quietly give up their own minds. But a real person must sometimes stand alone. Do not fear being misunderstood; "to be great is to be misunderstood." The great thinkers of the past were all doubted at first. So speak what you honestly believe today, even if it contradicts what you said yesterday.

## 参考译文 Translation

人在求学成长的某个阶段,会开始相信一件重要的事:他必须相信自己。在每个人的内心深处,都有一个只属于自己的声音、一种旁人不曾拥有的想法。要学会倾听它。那些我们敬仰的伟人,并非因模仿他人而伟大;他们之所以伟大,是因为他们相信自己的思想,并敢于将其大声说出。

我以为,嫉妒不过是一种无知,而模仿他人,便是在慢慢地毁掉自己。当你试图成为别人时,你就丢弃了那份唯独属于你的天赋。造化赋予我们每个人一处独特的位置、一份独特的使命,没有谁能做成你天生注定要做的那件事。倘若你终其一生都在模仿邻人,这世界便永远得不到那件唯有你才能奉献的东西。

最难的一点在于:社会并不希望你独立思考。它宁愿人人意见一致、遵循相同的习俗、附和相同的看法。为博得社会的好感,大多数人默默地交出了自己的头脑。然而,一个真正的人有时必须独自站立。不要害怕被误解——"伟大就意味着被误解"。往昔那些伟大的思想者,起初无不遭人怀疑。所以,把你今天真心相信的话说出来,哪怕它与你昨天所说的相抵触。

## 背景与理解 Notes

- **原作与作者**:《论自我》(又译《论自立》,Self-Reliance, 1841)是美国作家爱默生(Ralph Waldo Emerson)的名篇,他是超验主义的代表人物,文风以简练、格言警句式的句子著称。本段为面向学习者的改编转述,并非原文照录。
- **主旨**:相信自己与自己的思想;嫉妒源于无知,模仿则毁掉了你独有的东西;每个人都有他人无法替代的贡献;社会逼人从众,而真正的伟大在于独立思考。
- **结构 / 手法**:以断言与格言层层展开——相信自己、模仿即自毁、不惧被误解;由个人内心之声转向社会的从众压力。
- **语气**:自信、有力而鼓舞人心,带有挑战意味,督促读者独立自持。

## 阅读理解 Reading Comprehension

1. What is the main message of the passage?
- A) Always follow the opinions of the crowd
- B) Copy the great people of the past
- C) Trust yourself and think with your own mind
- D) Avoid all hard work

2. According to the writer, what were truly great people like?
- A) They copied the customs of others carefully
- B) They cared most about being popular
- C) They never disagreed with anyone
- D) They believed their own thoughts and dared to speak them

3. Why does the writer say society "does not want you to think for yourself"?
- A) Because society rewards independent thinking
- B) Because society pushes people to conform and give up their own minds
- C) Because most people think too much
- D) Because agreeing is always wrong

4. In the passage, "contradicts" most nearly means
- A) goes against
- B) repeats exactly
- C) agrees with
- D) explains clearly

5. The writer's tone throughout the passage is best described as
- A) confident and encouraging
- B) uncertain and fearful
- C) sad and hopeless
- D) angry and rude

**答案与解析 Answer Key**

1) **C** — 主旨题。全篇主张人"must trust himself",倾听内心"a private voice",独立思考,故选 B。
2) **D** — 细节题。原文说令人敬仰的伟人"believed their own thoughts and dared to speak them aloud"(相信自己的思想并敢于说出),故选 D。
3) **B** — 推断题。作者指出社会不愿你独立思考,更愿人人趋同,多数人"quietly give up their own minds",即社会施压使人从众,故选 A。
4) **A** — 词义题。"contradicts what you said yesterday"指今日之言与昨日相反,contradict 意为"相矛盾、相抵触",故选 C。
5) **A** — 语气·手法题。作者以格言式语句激励读者相信自己、勇于独立,语气自信而振奋,故选 B;其余选项均不符。
