## 原文选读 Passage

*— 改编自 The Yellow Wallpaper, Charlotte Perkins Gilman (1892)(公版作品). Adapted from a public-domain work.*

We have taken a big old house for the summer, and my husband, who is a doctor, says I must rest and do nothing at all. He is very kind, but he does not believe that I am truly ill. It is only my nerves, he says, and so I am not allowed to work, or to write, or even to think too much. Yet I write these lines in secret, when no one is near, because putting my thoughts on paper is the one thing that gives me relief.

They have put me in a large room at the top of the house. It has barred windows and a heavy bed, and around every wall runs the ugliest paper I have ever seen. It is a faded, unclean yellow, the colour of something old and sickly, and in places it has been torn away.

The pattern is the worst part. It curves and twists without any sense, and when I follow one line with my eye, it suddenly turns and destroys itself. The longer I look, the more it seems to move. My husband laughs at me and tells me not to give the room another thought. But I have little else to do, and so, hour after hour, I sit and study that strange, endless design upon the wall.

## 参考译文 Translation

今年夏天,我们租下了一座古老的大宅。我丈夫是医生,他说我必须休息,什么都不要做。他很体贴,却并不相信我是真的病了。他说这只是我神经的毛病,于是我不被允许工作、写作,甚至不许多想。可我还是趁没人在旁时偷偷写下这些文字,因为把心里的想法落在纸上,是唯一能让我感到宽慰的事。

他们把我安置在宅子顶层的一间大房间里。窗上装着栏杆,床又沉又重,四面墙上贴着我平生所见最丑的墙纸。那是一种褪了色的、脏兮兮的黄,像某种陈旧而病态之物的颜色,有些地方还被撕掉了。

最糟糕的是那图案。它毫无道理地弯曲、扭结;当我用目光去追一条线,它却突然一转,把自己毁得七零八落。我看得越久,它就越像在动。丈夫笑话我,叫我别再去想那房间。可我实在无事可做,于是一个钟头又一个钟头,我坐在那里,细看墙上那奇异而无尽的花纹。

## 背景与理解 Notes

- **原作与作者**:《黄色墙纸》(The Yellow Wallpaper, 1892)是美国作家 Charlotte Perkins Gilman(夏洛特·珀金斯·吉尔曼)的心理惊悚短篇,常被视为早期女性主义作品,批评当时强加于女性的"静养疗法"。本段为简化改编,并非原文照录。
- **主旨**:一位被要求"静养"、不许工作与写作的女性,在封闭的房间里日渐执迷于丑陋的黄色墙纸,揭示禁锢对心灵的压迫。
- **结构 / 手法**:第一人称日记体,采用"不可靠叙述者":她偷偷写作、丈夫不信她真的生病、墙纸"仿佛会动",都提示其感受与现实之间的落差;墙纸是核心象征。
- **语气**:表面平静、私密,底下却是压抑、焦虑与逐渐加深的执念。

## 阅读理解 Reading Comprehension

1. What is the passage mainly about?
- A) A woman who is told to rest and becomes fixated on the room's wallpaper
- B) A doctor who cures his wife of a serious illness
- C) A family choosing which house to buy for the summer
- D) A writer who publishes a famous book about wallpaper

2. What is the narrator not allowed to do?
- A) Leave her bed
- B) Speak to her husband
- C) Work or write
- D) Open the windows

3. Why does the narrator write "in secret"?
- A) She is planning to run away from the house.
- B) She has been forbidden to write but finds it a relief.
- C) She does not want her husband to read her poems.
- D) She is writing letters to a doctor in the city.

4. In "the colour of something old and sickly," the word *sickly* most nearly means
- A) very bright and fresh
- B) looking unhealthy or unpleasant
- C) sweet and pleasant
- D) hard and shining

5. When the narrator says the pattern "seems to move" and "destroys itself," this mainly suggests
- A) that the paper is truly alive
- B) that the whole house is falling down
- C) her uneasy, obsessive state of mind
- D) that other people have entered the room

**答案与解析 Answer Key**

1) **A** — 主旨题。叙述者被送去"休息",不许工作或写作,却"hour after hour"地盯着黄色墙纸看。医生从未治好她(B);房子是租来的,而非挑选购买(C);也没有出版任何书(D)。
2) **C** — 细节题。她明确说:"I am not allowed to work, or to write, or even to think too much."(不许工作、写作,甚至多想。)
3) **B** — 推断题。两处信息结合:她"not allowed... to write",可写作又"gives me relief",于是她"in secret, when no one is near"偷偷写。文中并无出逃(A)、写诗(C)或给城里医生写信(D)之意。
4) **B** — 词义题。这种黄"faded"(褪色)且"unclean"(肮脏),是"something old and sickly"的颜色,故 *sickly* 意为病态、看着不健康或令人不适,而非明亮、香甜或闪亮。
5) **C** — 手法·不可靠叙述题。墙纸不可能真的移动或"destroys itself";她久盯之下,墙纸只是"seems to move"。这指向她焦虑、执迷的心理状态,而非墙纸真的有生命(A)或房屋倒塌(B)。
