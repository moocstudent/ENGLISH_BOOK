## 听力原文 Transcript

Good afternoon, passengers. This is the final call for British Airways flight BA 249 to Madrid.
*各位旅客下午好。这是英国航空 BA 249 次飞往马德里航班的最后一次登机广播。*

The flight is now boarding at gate 32, and the gate will close at 2:20 this afternoon.
*该航班现在正在 32 号登机口登机,登机口将于今天下午 2:20 关闭。*

Please note that the gate has changed from gate 15 to gate 32, so please make your way there straight away.
*请注意,登机口已由 15 号变更为 32 号,请立即前往。*

Any passengers who are not on board by the closing time may not be able to travel.
*在关闭时间之前尚未登机的旅客可能将无法乘坐本次航班。*

Attention, please, on platform 4.
*请 4 号站台的旅客注意。*

We are sorry to announce that the 3:05 train to Manchester Piccadilly is delayed by 20 minutes.
*我们很抱歉地通知,3:05 开往曼彻斯特皮卡迪利站的列车晚点 20 分钟。*

The train will now depart from platform 4 at approximately 3:25 this afternoon.
*该列车现将于今天下午约 3:25 从 4 号站台发车。*

This delay is due to a signalling problem earlier on the line, and we apologise for any inconvenience.
*本次晚点是由于此前线路上的信号故障造成的,给您带来不便,我们深表歉意。*

Please check the departure boards for the latest information, and listen for further announcements.
*请查看出发信息屏获取最新信息,并留意后续广播。*

## 背景与理解 Notes

- **体裁**:机场与车站的公共广播(PA announcement),语速偏正式、句式固定。
- **要点信息**:抓住航班/车次编号、登机口/站台、时间,以及「延误 / 改登机口」这类变动。
- **听力策略**:注意 **final call**、**now boarding**、**delayed by…**、**depart from platform…** 等固定表达,以及和它们连在一起的数字。
