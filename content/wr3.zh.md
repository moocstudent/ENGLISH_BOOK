## 写作任务 Task

针对这句话写一段观点文字:「在家办公比在办公室办公更好。」请表明清晰立场,先写一句主题句,给出一两个理由和一个例子,最后用一句结论收尾。

*(60–120 词)*

## 评分要点 Success Criteria

- 第一句就亮明清晰的观点(主题句)
- 给出至少一个理由来支撑观点
- 包含一个具体的例子
- 用一句结论收尾,呼应开头观点

## 有用句型 Useful Phrases

- In my opinion, working from home is better than working in an office.
- I feel this way for two main reasons.
- One reason is that I save a lot of time on travelling.
- For example, last year I worked from home and got more done each day.
- However, some people find it harder to focus at home.
- Overall, I believe the benefits clearly outweigh the drawbacks.

## 参考范文 Model Answer

> In my opinion, working from home is better than working in an office. I feel this way for two main reasons. Firstly, I save a lot of time because I do not have to travel to work. For example, last year my commute took two hours a day, and now I use that time to rest or study. In addition, I can concentrate better in a quiet room. However, I understand that some people miss seeing their colleagues. Overall, I believe that working from home suits me best and makes me more productive.

*一段规范的观点文字:主题句表明立场 → 第一个理由(省下通勤时间)+ 例子 → 递进第二个理由(更专注)→ 一句让步转折 → 结论句呼应开头。*

## 提示 Notes

- **结构**:主题句(立场)→ 理由 + 例子 → 递进或让步 → 结论句。观点段要「首尾呼应」。
- **语气**:立场明确、有理有据;用 firstly / for example / however / overall 等衔接词把句子串起来。
