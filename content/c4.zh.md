## 原文选读 Passage

*— 改编自 Dracula, Bram Stoker (1897)(公版作品). Adapted from a public-domain work.*

The coach left me alone at the Borgo Pass just as the last light died behind the mountains. The other travellers had watched me with frightened eyes, and one old woman had pressed a small cross into my hand before we parted. Now the road was empty, and the cold air smelled of pine and snow.

Then, out of the darkness, a black carriage appeared. Four dark horses drew it, and the driver sat wrapped in a long cloak, a great hat hiding his face. When he turned to me, I saw only his bright, hard eyes and his lips, which were strangely red. "You are early tonight, my friend," he said, and his voice was deep and cold. He lifted me into the carriage with a hand that gripped like iron.

We climbed higher into the hills. The horses ran without rest, and the wind grew sharp. Somewhere in the black forest a wolf began to howl, and then another, until the whole night seemed alive with the sound. The horses trembled, but the driver only laughed softly to himself.

At last, against the grey sky, I saw the castle. It was huge and broken, its tall towers black against the stars, and not a single light burned in any window. The carriage stopped. I stood in the silent yard, my heart beating fast, and understood that I could not now turn back.

## 参考译文 Translation

就在最后一缕天光在群山背后消失时,马车把我一个人留在了博尔戈山口。同行的旅客都用惊恐的目光看着我,一位老妇人在分别前把一个小小的十字架塞进我手里。此刻道路空无一人,寒冷的空气里满是松树与积雪的气息。

随后,一辆黑色的马车从黑暗中出现。四匹黑马拉着它,车夫裹在长长的斗篷里,一顶大帽子遮住了他的脸。当他转向我时,我只看见他那双明亮而冷硬的眼睛,还有那红得出奇的嘴唇。"今晚你来得早啊,我的朋友,"他说,声音低沉而冰冷。他用一只像铁一样有力的手,把我抱上了马车。

我们朝山里越走越高。马不停蹄地奔跑,风也变得越来越凛冽。黑森林中某处,一只狼开始嗥叫,接着又是一只,直到整个夜晚仿佛都被这声音填满。马匹瑟瑟发抖,车夫却只是独自低声轻笑。

终于,在灰色天空的映衬下,我看见了那座城堡。它庞大而残破,高高的塔楼在星空下漆黑一片,没有一扇窗子透出灯光。马车停了下来。我站在寂静的院子里,心跳得飞快,明白自己此刻已无法回头。

## 背景与理解 Notes

- **原作与作者**:《德拉库拉》(Dracula, 1897)是爱尔兰作家 Bram Stoker(布拉姆·斯托克)的哥特恐怖代表作,全书由书信、日记等拼合而成(书信体)。本段改编自小说开头乔纳森·哈克(Jonathan Harker)前往城堡途中的日记,并非原文照录。
- **主旨**:借夜色、寒风、狼嗥与残破城堡,渲染主人公孤身深入险境、再难回头的不安与恐惧。
- **结构 / 手法**:第一人称日记体,按"被独自留下—车夫出现—山路夜行—抵达城堡"顺序推进;大量调动视觉、听觉、触觉细节(红唇、铁手、狼嗥)暗示车夫非同寻常。
- **语气**:压抑、紧张,弥漫不祥的预感;叙述冷静克制,却步步逼近恐惧。

## 阅读理解 Reading Comprehension

1. Which word best describes the overall mood of the passage?
- A) Cheerful and lively
- B) Dark and full of unease
- C) Calm and peaceful
- D) Busy and crowded

2. What had the old woman given the narrator before they parted?
- A) A warm coat
- B) A burning lamp
- C) A map of the road
- D) A small cross

3. What does the passage suggest about the driver?
- A) He is strange and unafraid, unlike a normal man.
- B) He is lost and does not know the road.
- C) He is a frightened, ordinary servant.
- D) He is an old friend the narrator has known for years.

4. In "the wind grew sharp," the word *sharp* most nearly means
- A) gentle
- B) sudden
- C) loud
- D) bitterly cold

5. How does the writer mainly create a feeling of dread?
- A) By describing bright colours and happy sounds
- B) By explaining the long history of the castle in detail
- C) Through darkness, howling wolves, and the silent ruined castle
- D) By listing the names of the other travellers

**答案与解析 Answer Key**

1) **B** — 主旨·情绪题。渐灭的天光、空荡的道路、诡异的车夫、狼嗥,以及"huge and broken"且无灯的城堡,共同营造出黑暗、不安的氛围。其余选项与冷清可怖的场景相悖;场景远非"peaceful",故 B 错。
2) **D** — 细节题。原文明确:老妇人"had pressed a small cross into my hand before we parted"(把一个小十字架塞进我手里)。
3) **A** — 推断题。车夫嘴唇"strangely red",手"gripped like iron";当马匹因狼嗥"trembled"时,他却"only laughed softly to himself"。这些细节表明他诡异而无所畏惧,并非普通或胆怯之人。
4) **D** — 词义题。越往高处爬,空气越冷,风也"grew sharp",故此处 *sharp* 意为刺骨、凛冽,而非"响亮"或"突然"。
5) **C** — 手法·语气题。恐惧感来自环境与声音:黑暗、狼嗥直到"the whole night seemed alive",以及"not a single light"的残破城堡。文中并未用明亮色彩、详述城堡历史,也未罗列人名。
