## Core Notes

### Prefixes: negation and degree

A **prefix** goes before the root and usually changes meaning, not word class.

Negation: **un-** (unhappy), **in-/im-/il-/ir-** (incorrect, impossible, illegal, irregular), **dis-** (disagree), **mis-** (misunderstand).

| Prefix | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| re- | again/back | rewrite, return |
| over- | too much | overwork, oversleep |
| under- | too little | underpaid, underestimate |
| pre- | before | preview, prepay |

*in-* becomes *im-* before m/p, *il-* before l, *ir-* before r (for easier pronunciation).

### Noun, adjective, verb suffixes

A **suffix** goes after the root and usually changes **word class**.

- Nouns: **-tion** (education), **-ment** (agreement), **-ness** (kindness), **-er/-or/-ist** (teacher, actor, artist).
- Adjectives: **-ful** (useful), **-less** (useless), **-able** (comfortable), **-ous** (famous), **-al** (national).
- Verbs: **-ize** (modernize), **-ify** (simplify), **-en** (widen).

Learn the chain: decide → decision → decisive → decisively.

### Compounds

Two words, one meaning; three spellings: solid (notebook), hyphen (well-known), open (bus stop). Stress usually falls on the first word: *GREENhouse* ≠ green *HOUSE*.

### Roots and cognates

Roots carry the core meaning:

| Root | Meaning | Words |
|---|---|---|
| port | carry | export, portable |
| spect | look | inspect, respect |
| dict | say | predict, dictate |
| vis/vid | see | visible, video |

Beware **false friends** — words that look familiar but mean something else.

### Vocabulary notebook method

Organize by **word family**, not single words. For each entry add your own example, the collocation, and the register. Review with spaced repetition (1, 3, 7, 14 days).

### Summary

Prefixes change meaning; suffixes change class. Learn chains and roots, and watch for false friends.

## Exercises

1. Add prefixes: legal, regular, honest, understand.
2. *develop* → noun; *wide* → verb.
3. Fix: *This app is very use.* → **useful**
4. Give two words from the root vis/vid.
5. Sort spelling: notebook, well-known, bus stop.
6. Is *invaluable* = worthless?

**Answer key.** 1) illegal, irregular, dishonest, misunderstand  2) development; widen  3) useful  4) visible, video (etc.)  5) solid; hyphen; open  6) No — it means extremely valuable.
