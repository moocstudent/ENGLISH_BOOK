## Transcript

**A:** Hello, come in and take a seat. What seems to be the problem today?

**B:** Hi, doctor. I've had a really sore throat and a headache for the last three days.

**A:** I'm sorry to hear that. Have you had a fever at all?

**B:** A little. The nurse just took my temperature and it was 37.8 degrees.

**A:** Okay, that's only slightly high. Are you coughing, or is it mainly your throat?

**B:** Mostly my throat. It really hurts when I swallow, especially in the morning.

**A:** Let me have a quick look. Open wide and say 'ah'... Yes, it's a bit red, but nothing serious.

**B:** That's a relief. Do I need antibiotics?

**A:** No, I don't think so. It looks like a common cold, and that usually clears up on its own.

**A:** Get plenty of rest, drink lots of water, and you can take paracetamol for the pain.

**B:** How often should I take it?

**A:** One or two tablets every six hours, but no more than eight in a day.

**A:** If you're not better in a week, or the fever gets higher, come back and see me.

**B:** Thank you, doctor. I'll do that.
